Best Dog Parks in Austin, TX

An amenity-first look at Austin’s official dog off-leash areas (DOLAs) — fencing, water, small-dog space, hours, and what the city actually requires.

Austin Parks and Recreation designates Dog Off-Leash Areas (DOLAs) as the only public places in the city where dogs may legally be unrestrained. The city’s published off-leash inventory has grown to roughly 30 mapped areas (February 2026 city map), ranging from large unfenced metro-park tracts to small fenced neighborhood runs.

No special off-leash permit or tag is required to use city DOLAs. Texas law still requires current rabies vaccination. Dogs must be leashed when walking to and from the off-leash zone. General park hours are 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. unless a park posts otherwise (Emma Long Metropolitan Park opens at 7 a.m.).

Below we highlight 10 well-documented parks with verified amenity fields. Unknowns are labeled rather than guessed. Addresses come from the city map and park pages — see sources.md.

Lady Bird Lake algae advisory Austin Watershed Protection warns that blue-green algae toxins in Lady Bird Lake (and sometimes other waterways) can harm or kill dogs. Prefer shore walks at Red Bud Isle and Auditorium Shores; check austintexas.gov/algae before any swim, and bring fresh drinking water.

West Austin Neighborhood Park Fenced

1317 W 10th St · Clarksville / West Austin

  • Fenced Yes — fully fenced off-leash section
  • Water Drinking water / stations reported on site
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate large & small sections
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (general park hours)

Compact neighborhood favorite; off-leash only inside the fenced run. Rest of the park (playground, pool area) is leash-required.

Riverside Shores Fenced

~1016 Edgecliff Terrace · South shore, Lady Bird Lake

  • Fenced Yes — fully fenced off-leash area
  • Water Lake shoreline nearby — algae caution; fountain unknown
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate large & small play areas
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (general park hours)

Often listed near / alongside the Norwood Tract area. Good pick when you need a fence close to downtown. Confirm posted signs for exact boundaries.

Auditorium Shores Partly fenced

~920–1100 W Riverside Dr · South Central, Lady Bird Lake

  • Fenced Partly — not fully enclosed
  • Water Lake access + dog drinking fountain / rinse area (redesign)
  • Small-dog area No dedicated small-dog section verified
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (general park hours)

Popular downtown-view lawn. Keep dogs out of Lady Bird Lake when algae advisories are active. Leash up outside the marked off-leash zone (busy trail & road nearby).

Red Bud Isle

3401 Red Bud Trl · West Austin, Lady Bird Lake

  • Fenced No — open island / wooded shoreline
  • Water Lake shoreline — strong algae caution
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (general park hours)

~13-acre wooded island reached by bridge; entire designated area is off-leash. Excellent for explorers with solid recall. Bring fresh water; do not let dogs drink lake water during algae events.

Zilker Park — Great Lawn

2100 Barton Springs Rd · Zilker / South Central

  • Fenced No — open grassy off-leash zone
  • Water Unknown / not verified for this lawn (bring your own)
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m.; closures for ACL Fest, Trail of Lights, etc.

Large central fetch field (~46 acres on city map). Expect crowds on weekends; check park closure notices before festival seasons. Weekend/holiday parking fees may apply seasonally.

Barking Springs at Zilker Swim

2105 Andrew Zilker Rd · Below Barton Springs Pool spillway

  • Fenced No
  • Water Yes — spring-fed creek / spillway swim (not Barton Springs Pool)
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (within Zilker hours)

Classic Austin dog swim spot. Dogs are not allowed in Barton Springs Pool itself. City has found toxic algae in nearby backwater — check algae updates before swimming.

Walnut Creek Metropolitan Park

12138 N Lamar Blvd · North Austin

  • Fenced No — large unfenced wooded / trail acreage
  • Water Creek access in parts of the off-leash zone
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (general park hours)

One of Austin’s largest DOLAs (~185 acres on city map). Best for dogs with reliable recall; trails are shared. Read boundary signs — some picnic / pool areas stay leash-required.

Emma Long Metropolitan Park (Turkey Creek Trail)

1711 City Park Rd · Far West / Lake Austin hills

  • Fenced No — designated trail / bluff section
  • Water Creek crossings along Turkey Creek Trail
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours Park open 7 a.m. – 10 p.m. (later start than most parks)

Largest area on the city off-leash map (~193 acres). Main park day-use fees apply elsewhere ($5 Mon–Thu / $10 Fri–Sun & holidays per vehicle per city page); Turkey Creek Trail parking is commonly described as before the pay station — verify on arrival. Note: park rules page also says pets on leash at all times; follow posted DOLA signage.

Shoal Creek Greenbelt

~2610 Shoal Creek Blvd / Lamar corridor · Central

  • Fenced No — trail / greenbelt off-leash stretch
  • Water Unknown — bring drinking water
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (general park hours)

More trail than enclosed park; sits near a busy road. Only suitable for dogs with strong recall. Confirm marked DOLA boundaries before unclipping.

Mary Moore Searight Metropolitan Park

907 W Slaughter Ln · South Austin

  • Fenced No — unfenced trail / open-space DOLA
  • Water Unknown — bring drinking water
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. (general park hours)

Large south-Austin metro park with a substantial off-leash portion (~39 acres on city map). Dogs welcome on-leash elsewhere in the park.

FAQ — Austin dog parks

Do dogs need to be on a leash in Austin parks?

Yes, except inside a designated Dog Off-Leash Area. Austin City Code requires dogs on a leash in public areas unless the location is designated otherwise. Leash your dog when walking to and from the off-leash zone, and keep dogs within sight and voice control while off-leash.

What are the typical hours?

Austin parks are generally open 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. unless otherwise posted. Emma Long Metropolitan Park opens at 7 a.m. Events, festivals, and maintenance can close or limit access — check signage and city notices (especially at Zilker).

Is there a permit or registration for off-leash areas?

No special city off-leash permit or tag is required for public DOLAs. Rabies vaccination is required by Texas state law. Secondary guides also note city DOLA guidelines such as a maximum of three off-leash dogs per handler and carrying a leash (often cited as 6 ft or less) for entry/exit — always follow posted rules at the park.

What should I bring?

Leash (required outside the DOLA), waste bags, fresh drinking water and a bowl (especially in summer heat), and shade/cooling plans for May–September. Do not rely on Lady Bird Lake for drinking or swimming when algae advisories are in effect. Flat-faced breeds overheat faster — go early.

Can my dog swim at Red Bud Isle or Auditorium Shores?

Both are still great for walks, but Austin attributes multiple dog deaths since 2019 to blue-green algae toxins in Lady Bird Lake. Check austintexas.gov/algae before any water contact. If a dog gets in, rinse with fresh water and contact a vet if symptoms appear. Barking Springs (Barton Creek spillway) is the classic swim alternative — still check algae updates for nearby backwater.

What are the basic off-leash rules?

Per Austin Parks and Recreation: leash to/from the area; keep dogs in sight and under sound control; no dogs in heat; remove aggressive dogs immediately; rabies vaccinations required; clean up waste and use a trash can.
