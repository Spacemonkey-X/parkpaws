Best Dog Parks in Fort Worth, TX

An amenity-first look at Fort Worth Park & Recreation off-leash dog parks — fencing, water, small-dog space, hours, maintenance days, and city rules.

Fort Worth Park & Recreation lists six off-leash dog parks. Two are destination parks with separate large- and small-dog areas — Fort Woof in Gateway Park (5 acres) and ZBonz on the former Z. Boaz Golf Course (10 acres, with ponds). The other four — Calhound, Bark Park at First Flight, Fire Station, and Palmilla Springs — are small fenced neighborhood runs.

City dog park rules include: licensed dogs with current rabies tags (Fort Worth residents need city license tags); leash on until you are inside the double gate; a visible leash at all times; small-dog areas are for dogs under 40 lb; no puppies under four months; no dogs in season; no food or drink inside; fill holes your dog digs; adults must accompany children under 12. Posted hours are 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. unless otherwise posted.

Below: 6 city dog parks with amenity fields from fortworthtexas.gov park pages. Unknowns are labeled. See sources.md.

Check maintenance days and rain closures Fort Woof closes every Thursday and ZBonz every Wednesday for scheduled maintenance, and both can close after rain when the ground is saturated. Check the city dog parks page before you drive.

Fort Woof Dog Park (Gateway Park) Fenced

751 Beach St, Fort Worth, TX 76111 · East Fort Worth / Gateway Park · Fort Worth Park & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — 5 fenced acres (3.4-acre large-dog area + 1.6-acre small-dog area); double-gate entry
  • Water Yes — water fountains listed
  • Small-dog area Yes — 1.6-acre area for dogs under 40 lb
  • Hours Fri–Wed 5 a.m. – 10 p.m.; closed Thu for maintenance; may close after rain

The city’s first off-leash dog park (opened April 3, 2004) inside Gateway Park, near the softball fields. Agility courses, shaded shelters, waste stations, and picnic tables listed. Gateway Park also has restrooms, trails, and parking.

ZBonz Dog Park Largest

6950 W Camp Bowie Blvd, Fort Worth, TX 76116 · West Fort Worth / former Z. Boaz Golf Course · Fort Worth Park & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — 10 acres, fully irrigated; double-gate entry
  • Water Yes — pet fountains; two aerated ponds
  • Small-dog area Yes — 3-acre small-dog section (7-acre large-dog section)
  • Hours Thu–Tue 5 a.m. – 10 p.m.; closed Wed for maintenance; may close after rain

Built on the former Z. Boaz Golf Course. Two shelters, agility items, and picnic tables listed. Ponds are aerated — judge water yourself and rinse dogs after a dip.

Calhound Dog Park (Calhoun Park) Urban

409 E Annie St, Fort Worth, TX 76104 · Near Southside / South Main · Fort Worth Park & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — small fenced area
  • Water Unknown — not listed on city page
  • Small-dog area Unknown — no separate area listed
  • Hours Daily 5:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.

Small urban dog park inside Calhoun Park near the South Main area. City lists covered seating / patio.

Bark Park at First Flight Small dogs

2700 Mercedes Ave, Fort Worth, TX 76107 · West 7th / Cultural District · Fort Worth Park & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — about 3,200 sq ft fenced area
  • Water Unknown — not listed on city page
  • Small-dog area Yes — whole park recommended for small dogs only (large dogs not recommended)
  • Hours Tue–Sun 5:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.; Mon 11 a.m. – 10 p.m.

Tiny urban bark park in First Flight Park near West 7th Street. Picnic tables listed. Best for small or calm dogs.

Fire Station Dog Park Neighborhood

1601 Lipscomb St, Fort Worth, TX 76104 · Fairmount · Fort Worth Park & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — small fenced area
  • Water Unknown — not listed on city page
  • Small-dog area Unknown — no separate area listed
  • Hours Daily 5:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.

In Fire Station Park near Hemphill & Allen in Fairmount, next to a skatepark, playground, and community center. Picnic tables listed.

Palmilla Springs Dog Park Neighborhood

2957 Plumbago Dr, Fort Worth, TX 76108 · Far West Fort Worth (north of I-30, west of Chapel Creek Blvd) · Fort Worth Park & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — small fenced area
  • Water Unknown — not listed on city page
  • Small-dog area Unknown — no separate area listed
  • Hours Daily 5:30 a.m. – 10 p.m.

Inside Palmilla Springs Park. Dog area has a bench and pet waste station; the park adds a paved walking trail, playground, covered pavilion, and picnic tables.

FAQ — Fort Worth dog parks

What is the best dog park in Fort Worth?

For space and amenities, ZBonz (10 acres, two aerated ponds, pet fountains, agility items) and Fort Woof (5 acres, agility courses, water fountains, shaded shelters) are the city’s two large parks. Both have separate small-dog areas.

Which Fort Worth dog parks have a small-dog area?

Fort Woof (1.6 acres) and ZBonz (3 acres) have separate small-dog sections for dogs under 40 lb. Bark Park at First Flight is about 3,200 sq ft and the city recommends it for small dogs only. The city pages do not list separate small-dog areas at Calhound, Fire Station, or Palmilla Springs.

When are Fort Woof and ZBonz closed?

Per the city park pages, Fort Woof is closed Thursdays and ZBonz is closed Wednesdays for scheduled maintenance; other days run 5 a.m. – 10 p.m. Either park may also close after rainfall if the ground is too saturated.

Do I need a Fort Worth dog license to use the parks?

Dogs must be licensed and wear current rabies tags. Dogs owned by Fort Worth residents must have city license tags issued by Animal Services.

Which park has water for dogs to swim in?

ZBonz lists two aerated ponds. Judge water quality yourself and rinse dogs afterward. The city pages do not list swim water at the other parks.

What should I bring?

Leash (you must have a visible one at all times), waste bags, fresh water and a bowl — the four smaller parks don’t list water — and a towel for ZBonz pond days. North Texas summers are hot, so go early or late.
