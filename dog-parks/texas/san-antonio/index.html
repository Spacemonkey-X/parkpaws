Best Dog Parks in San Antonio, TX

An amenity-first look at San Antonio’s official off-leash dog parks — fencing, water, small-dog space, hours, and Parks & Recreation rules.

San Antonio Parks and Recreation operates about 21–22 off-leash dog parks citywide (the Dog Parks Directory lists 22, including two at Phil Hardberger Park and two at Tom Slick Park). Unlike Austin’s many unfenced “off-leash areas,” San Antonio’s public dog parks are typically enclosed runs inside larger parks.

No special dog-park permit is described on the city Dog Parks page. Dogs must be at least 4 months old and fully vaccinated; handlers may bring no more than 2 dogs; keep a leash ready; dogs in heat stay home; clean up waste; leave food, treats, and toys at home (Hardberger Conservancy notes training treats are allowed there). Most parks are open 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.; some dog areas (Hardberger, Panther Springs, New Territories) run sunrise to sunset.

Below: 10 well-documented parks with verified amenity fields. Unknowns are labeled. Addresses and hours come from sa.gov park pages — see sources.md.

Heat & shade South Texas summers are intense. Prefer early morning or evening visits, bring extra water even when fountains are listed, and confirm fountain operation on site — city pages do not guarantee every fountain runs year-round.

Phil Hardberger Park — West (NW Military) Fenced

8400 NW Military Hwy · North / Wurzbach Parkway corridor

  • Fenced Yes — shaded, enclosed dog park
  • Water Yes — drinking fountains (conservancy + city)
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate small & all-dog sections (~1.5 acres)
  • Hours Sunrise – sunset (dog parks); park trails sunrise – sunset

City notes agility equipment on the west side. Dogs must be leashed on park trails outside the enclosure. Often cited among SA’s best shaded runs.

Phil Hardberger Park — East (Blanco Rd) Fenced

13203 Blanco Rd · North Central

  • Fenced Yes — shaded, enclosed dog park
  • Water Yes — drinking fountains
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate small & all-dog sections (~1.8 acres)
  • Hours Sunrise – sunset

City notes a two-story doghouse in the small-dog area (donor feature). Pair with west-side Hardberger if you want a second option nearby.

Tom Slick Park Fenced

7400 State Hwy 151 · Northwest

  • Fenced Yes — two fenced dog enclosures on site
  • Water Yes — fountain for people & pets; doggie shower
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate large & small areas (Hwy 151 entrance, ~1 acre)
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.

City: second fenced enclosure at the Timber Creek entrance near pavilion/playground. Shade structures and solar lights noted as pending on the city page.

Maverick Park Fenced

1000 Broadway · Near downtown / Tobin Hill

  • Fenced Yes — one-acre dog park (opened 2021)
  • Water Yes — drinking fountain with pet fountain
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate all-dog & small-dog sections
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.

City lists mutt-mitt dispensers, picnic seating, and a Portland Loo restroom among enhancements. Compact urban option with true size separation.

Madison Square Park

400 Lexington Ave · Downtown / Midtown

  • Fenced Yes — fenced off-leash area (~0.65 acre)
  • Water Yes — doggie drinking fountain
  • Small-dog area No separate section stated on city page
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.

Opened 2010. Convenient downtown run with benches and mutt-mitts. For a nearby split large/small yard, Maverick is a few blocks away.

McAllister Park

13102 Jones Maltsberger Rd · North (near airport); dog park near Starcrest entrance

  • Fenced Yes — 2.42 acres of fenced land
  • Water Unknown in enclosure — park lists water fountains; bring water
  • Small-dog area Unknown — not specified on city dog-park blurb
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 11 p.m. (city: no lighting in the dog park)

City: exercise equipment, covered picnic area, benches, walking trail inside the dog park. Large acreage relative to most SA dog parks.

Pearsall Park

5102 Old Pearsall Rd · Southwest

  • Fenced Yes — 1.5-acre dog park (city dog-park listing)
  • Water Park lists water fountain — dog-park fountain not separately confirmed
  • Small-dog area No — city: “Combined Large & Small Dog Area”
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.

First city dog park (opened 2005 per local reporting). Big destination park with trails, skate park, and other amenities outside the dog run.

Panther Springs Park

22635 Wilderness Oak · Far North / Stone Oak area

  • Fenced Yes — 1.5-acre dog park near main entrance
  • Water Park lists water fountain — enclosure fountain not separately confirmed
  • Small-dog area No — city: “Combined Large & Small Dog Area”
  • Hours Dog park: sunrise – sunset (city); park page also lists sunrise – sunset

Useful far-north option. City copy mentions areas for big and small dogs to play, but the size field is labeled combined — treat as one shared yard unless signs say otherwise.

New Territories Park Fenced

9023 Bowen Dr · Northwest

  • Fenced Yes — ~0.25 acre dog park near main entrance
  • Water Park lists water fountain — enclosure fountain not separately confirmed
  • Small-dog area Yes — city: “Separate Large & Small Dog Areas”
  • Hours Dog park: sunrise – sunset; park grounds 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.

Compact neighborhood park with true size separation. Confirm dog-park gate hours on the posted sign (city dog-park hours are sunrise–sunset).

Woodlawn Lake Park

1103 Cincinnati Ave · Near West Side

  • Fenced Yes — listed as a city dog park (enclosed SA system); size unknown
  • Water Park lists water fountain — dog-park details limited on city page
  • Small-dog area Unknown — not described on city park page
  • Hours Park: 5 a.m. – 12 a.m. (midnight); confirm dog-park gate posting

City amenities list includes Dog Park. Featured amenity section does not expand dog-park fencing/size — visit for posted rules. Lake/park trails are separate from the dog run.

FAQ — San Antonio dog parks

How many dog parks does San Antonio have?

The city’s Dog Parks Directory states 22 off-leash dog parks, including two at Phil Hardberger Park and two at Tom Slick Park. The parent Dog Parks page currently says 21 — treat the directory count as the inventory to browse, and verify a specific park is open before you go.

What are the city dog park rules?

From San Antonio Parks & Recreation: supervise your dogs; no more than 2 dogs per handler; always have a leash ready; dogs in heat stay home; dogs must be at least 4 months old and fully vaccinated; clean up waste; leave food, treats, and toys at home; remove aggressive dogs immediately; owners are legally responsible for injuries. Hardberger Conservancy adds collar/license-tag expectations and allows training treats at that park.

What are typical hours?

Many parks list 5 a.m. – 11 p.m. daily. Phil Hardberger dog parks are sunrise to sunset. Panther Springs and New Territories dog parks are also posted sunrise–sunset on their city pages. Woodlawn Lake Park grounds list 5 a.m. – midnight — check the dog-park gate sign.

Do I need a permit?

The city Dog Parks page does not require a special off-leash permit. Vaccinations (and, per Hardberger/local guidance, current license tags) are expected. Bring proof if asked and keep tags on the collar.

Which parks have separate small-dog areas?

Verified on city or conservancy pages: Phil Hardberger (both sides), Tom Slick (Hwy 151 entrance), Maverick, and New Territories. Madison Square is a single fenced yard. Pearsall and Panther Springs are labeled combined large & small. McAllister and Woodlawn: unknown from city copy.

What should I bring?

Leash (required entering/leaving), waste bags, fresh water and a bowl (backup even if a pet fountain is listed), shade/cooling plans for summer, and current vaccine/license info. Leave toys and people food at home per city rules.
