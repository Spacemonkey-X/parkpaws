## Amenity verification notes

| Park | Fenced | Water | Small-dog | Hours / closures | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barry Annino / Bark Park Central | Yes (1.2 ac) | Bowl + dog shower | No (FAQ exception) | Mon/Wed–Sun 6–11; closed Tue + rain | Deep Ellum / downtown |
| White Rock / Mockingbird Point | Yes (3 ac; 6′ fence) | Fountains, washes, dog launch | Yes (1 ac small / 2 ac large) | Tue–Sun 5–11; closed Mon + rain | First city dog park |
| Wagging Tail | Yes (6.9 ac) | Bowl + fountain | Yes | Tue–Sun 5–11; closed Mon + rain | Observation deck; loop trail |
| NorthBark | Yes (22.3 ac) | Doggy lake/beach, shower, bowl, fountain | Yes | Wed–Mon 5–11; closed Tue + rain | Largest in North Texas (city) |
| Crockett | Yes (1.25 ac; 4′ fence) | Bowl + fountain | Yes (large + small paddocks) | Closed Mon + rain; open hours Unknown on page | Old East Dallas |
| Parkhill | Yes (1 ac) | Dog drinking bowl | Unknown on feature list | Wed–Mon 5–11; closed Tue + rain | On Parkhill Jr High campus |

City FAQ (dog-parks page): with the exception of Bark Park Central, parks have dedicated small- and large-dog areas. Parkhill’s facility feature list does not name separate paddocks — marked Unknown on the hub.
