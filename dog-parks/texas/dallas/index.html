Best Dog Parks in Dallas, TX

An amenity-first look at Dallas Park & Recreation off-leash dog parks — fencing, water, small-dog space, hours, rain closures, and city rules.

Dallas Park and Recreation maintains designated off-leash dog parks across downtown, East Dallas, and North Dallas. The department’s dog-parks overview highlights four headline sites (Bark Park Central / Barry Annino, Mockingbird Point / White Rock Lake, Wagging Tail, and NorthBark). Facility pages also document Crockett and Parkhill dog parks.

City off-leash rules (Dallas City Ordinance #24482 / posted site rules) include: max three dogs per person; no aggressive dogs or females in heat; rabies tags (and Dallas registration for city residents); leash when entering/leaving; remove pinch/prong/spiked collars inside the fence; no food or treats; pick up waste; keep visual and voice control; report bites to Animal Control. Children under 12 must be under strict supervision — parents are strongly discouraged from bringing them in.

Below: 6 well-documented city dog parks with verified amenity fields from dallasparks.org facility pages. Unknowns are labeled. See sources.md.

Rain-out / Dog Park Hotline — 214-614-6711 Dallas dog parks close on rain days and on each park’s weekly maintenance day. Call the Dog Park Hotline / Rain Out Line at 214-614-6711 (or use the Rain Out Line app) before you go.

Barry Annino Bark Park (Bark Park Central) Downtown

2445 Canton St, Dallas, TX 75201 · Deep Ellum / Downtown · Dallas Parks

  • Fenced Yes — 1.2 acre off-leash enclosure
  • Water Yes — dog drinking bowl; dog shower
  • Small-dog area No — city FAQ exception (no separate small/large split)
  • Hours Mon, Wed–Sun 6 a.m. – 11 p.m.; closed Tue for maintenance and on rain days

Urban park under elevated US-75 at Good-Latimer & Commerce, with downtown views and dog murals. Walking distance to Deep Ellum. Shaded areas and pet waste stations listed.

White Rock Lake Dog Park (Mockingbird Point) Fenced

8000 Mockingbird Ln, Dallas, TX 75218 · East Dallas / White Rock Lake · Dallas Parks

  • Fenced Yes — 3 acres total; 6′ ornamental fencing; gated paddocks
  • Water Yes — drinking fountains; dog washes; dog-launch water access & overlook
  • Small-dog area Yes — ~1 acre small / ~2 acres large paddocks
  • Hours Tue–Sun 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.; closed Mon for maintenance and on rain days

First dog park in the Dallas Park system (Mockingbird Point). Renovated site on the northwest side of White Rock Lake with pavilion, shade structures, benches, and parking.

Wagging Tail Dog Park Fenced

5841 Keller Springs Rd, Dallas, TX 75248 · North Dallas · Dallas Parks

  • Fenced Yes — 6.9 acre off-leash park; metal fencing with dog silhouettes
  • Water Yes — dog drinking bowl; drinking fountain
  • Small-dog area Yes — large & small dog areas listed
  • Hours Tue–Sun 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.; closed Mon for maintenance and on rain days

Northwest of Preston Rd. Flagstone observation deck ~35 ft above White Rock Creek with waterfall views; 6′ loop trail; shaded areas, benches, picnic tables, parking.

NorthBark Dog Park Fenced

4899 Gramercy Oaks, Dallas, TX 75287 · Far North Dallas · Dallas Parks

  • Fenced Yes — 22.3 acre off-leash park (largest in North Texas per city)
  • Water Yes — doggy lake / beach (pond open per city); dog shower; drinking bowl & fountain
  • Small-dog area Yes — large & small dog areas listed
  • Hours Wed–Mon 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.; closed Tue for maintenance and on rain days

Near Dallas North Tollway & PGBT. Trails, pavilion, picnic tables, portalets, parking, and metal fencing with dog silhouettes. Confirm pond status on arrival.

Crockett Dog Park Neighborhood

501 N Carroll Ave, Dallas, TX 75246 · Old East Dallas · Dallas Parks

  • Fenced Yes — 1.25 acres; 4′ ornamental fencing; gated paddock entries
  • Water Yes — dog drinking bowl; drinking fountain
  • Small-dog area Yes — large & small paddocks (small with mesh fencing)
  • Hours Closed Mon for maintenance and on rain days; open-hour window Unknown on facility page

Neighborhood-scale park at Victor St & N Carroll Ave, near the Medical District. Benches, waste stations, plaza area, and site lighting listed. Cultural Affairs artwork noted as coming soon on the facility page.

Parkhill Dog Park Fenced

16500 Shadybank Dr, Dallas, TX 75248 · North Dallas · Dallas Parks

  • Fenced Yes — 1 acre off-leash park
  • Water Yes — dog drinking bowl
  • Small-dog area Unknown on facility feature list (city FAQ says all parks except Bark Park Central have separate areas — confirm on site)
  • Hours Wed–Mon 5 a.m. – 11 p.m.; closed Tue for maintenance and on rain days

Located on the Parkhill Junior High School campus (Council District 12). Shaded areas, benches, and pet waste station listed.

FAQ — Dallas dog parks

Do dogs need to be leashed in Dallas parks?

Yes, except inside a designated off-leash dog park. Per posted site rules and Dallas City Code, dogs must be on leash when entering and exiting an off-leash site; once inside, dogs are unleashed and run free under visual and voice control.

What are the city dog park rules?

From Dallas Parks off-leash rules: no dangerous/aggressive dogs; no females in heat; max three dogs per person; rabies vaccination tags required (Dallas registration for residents); remove pinch/prong/spiked collars before entering; no food or treats (dog or human); no glass; pick up waste; fill holes your dog digs; keep children under 12 under strict supervision. Use of the site implies waiver of liability to the City (Ordinance #24482). Violations may carry fines up to $2,000.

Do parks have separate small- and large-dog areas?

Dallas Parks FAQ states that, with the exception of Bark Park Central (Barry Annino), dog parks have dedicated small- and large-dog areas. Facility pages confirm splits at White Rock / Mockingbird Point, Wagging Tail, NorthBark, and Crockett. Parkhill’s feature list does not name separate paddocks — confirm on site.

When are parks closed for rain or maintenance?

All listed parks close on rain days. Weekly maintenance closures vary: Barry Annino and NorthBark / Parkhill close Tuesdays; White Rock, Wagging Tail, and Crockett close Mondays. Call the Dog Park Hotline / Rain Out Line at 214-614-6711 before you drive.

Which parks have swimming or a pond?

NorthBark lists a doggy lake / beach (pond noted as open on the facility page). White Rock Lake Dog Park lists dog-launch water access and overlook toward the lake. Always judge water quality yourself and rinse dogs after swims.

What should I bring?

Leash, waste bags, fresh water and a bowl (backup for broken fountains), towels if visiting pond/launch parks, and current tags/vaccine info. North Texas heat is serious — prefer early morning or evening, and check the rain-out line after storms.
