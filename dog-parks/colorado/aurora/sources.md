# Sources — Aurora, CO dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Aurora city hub (Denver Metro). Facilities and amenity flags come from official city (or operating-agency) pages; gaps are marked **unknown**. Secondary sources are used only to cross-check and are labeled on the page.

## Primary (official)

- City of Aurora — Dog Parks / Dog Training (Bicentennial, Grandview, Singletree details; 303-739-7160):  
  https://www.auroragov.org/things_to_do/recreation___sports_programs/dog_parks__dog_training
- City of Aurora — Park listing (Grandview 5.7 ac; Bicentennial and Singletree off-leash features; 5 a.m.–11 p.m. park hours):  
  https://www.auroragov.org/things_to_do/parks__open_space___trails/park_listing
- Colorado Parks & Wildlife — Cherry Creek State Park activities (dog off-leash area, passes, fencing):  
  https://cpw.state.co.us/state-parks/cherry-creek-state-park/cherry-creek-state-park-activities-trails
