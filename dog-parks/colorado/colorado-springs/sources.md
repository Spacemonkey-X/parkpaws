# Sources — Colorado Springs, CO dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Colorado Springs city hub (Pikes Peak Region). Facilities and amenity flags come from official city (or operating-agency) pages; gaps are marked **unknown**. Secondary sources are used only to cross-check and are labeled on the page.

## Primary (official)

- City of Colorado Springs — Dog Parks (fenced vs unfenced list, rules):  
  https://coloradosprings.gov/parks/page/dog-parks
- Antlers Dog Park (two areas, double gates, turf, water station):  
  https://parks.coloradosprings.gov/AntlersDogPark
- Garden of the Gods dog map & policies (hours, unenclosed run, rules):  
  https://coloradosprings.gov/system/files/2026-05/Dog%20Map.pdf
- Red Rock Canyon Open Space (dog loops, amenities):  
  https://www.coloradosprings.gov/redrockcanyon
- El Paso County — Bear Creek Dog Park (25 ac, small-dog area, water, hours):  
  https://parks.elpasoco.com/parks-and-recreation/bear-creek-dog-park/

## Secondary corroboration
