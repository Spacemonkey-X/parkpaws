Best Dog Parks in Boulder, CO

An amenity-first look at Boulder’s three fenced City dog parks and the Howard Heuston off-leash area — acreage, small-dog yards, water, and Voice & Sight rules.

The City of Boulder runs three fenced dog parks — East Boulder, Foothills, and Valmont — each with separate large- and small-dog areas, plus one off-leash dog area at Howard Heuston Park that works differently.

Dog parks are free and open dawn to dusk. City ordinances apply inside, leash laws apply on the way in and out, aggressive dogs are prohibited, and guardians must pick up after their dogs.

Howard Heuston is a Voice and Sight area: only people registered in the City’s Voice and Sight Tag Program may have dogs off leash there, and the tag must be visible. See sources.md.

Howard Heuston needs a Voice and Sight Tag The off-leash zone at Howard Heuston (34th & Iris south to O’Neal Pkwy) is only north of the partial fence, about 1.25 acres. Leashes are required in the rest of the park, and you must be registered in Boulder’s Voice and Sight Tag Program to let your dog off leash.

East Boulder Dog Park Swim

5660 Sioux Dr. · East Boulder Community Park

  • Fenced Yes — fenced large (~1.5 ac) and small (~0.45 ac) areas (City)
  • Water Limited access to a small lake (City)
  • Small-dog area Yes — about 0.45 acre (City)
  • Hours Dawn to dusk (City)

City notes a concrete barrier in the lake that some dogs can climb — watch swimmers.

Foothills Dog Park Fenced

800 Cherry Ave. · Foothills Community Park (north Boulder)

  • Fenced Yes — 2-acre fenced park (City)
  • Water Unknown — not listed by the City
  • Small-dog area Yes — about 0.5 acre; large area about 1.6 acres (City)
  • Hours Dawn to dusk (City)

Free. Bring a bowl and water.

Valmont Dog Park Fenced

Approx. 5300 Valmont Rd · Valmont City Park

  • Fenced Yes — fenced large (~3 ac) and small (~0.25 ac) areas (City)
  • Water Water spigot available seasonally (City)
  • Small-dog area Yes — about 0.25 acre (City)
  • Hours Dawn to dusk (City)

Boulder’s largest large-dog yard. Valmont City Park also has a disc golf course.

Howard Heuston Off-Leash Dog Area Voice & Sight

34th St. & Iris Ave., south to O’Neal Pkwy · Howard Heuston Park

  • Fenced Partial — off-leash zone is north of the partial fence only (City)
  • Water Unknown
  • Small-dog area No
  • Hours Not stated separately on the City page

About 1.25 acres. Voice and Sight Tag required and visibly displayed; dogs leashed in the rest of the park. Park also has a playground, skate area, and basketball court.

FAQ — Boulder dog parks

Are Boulder dog parks free?

Yes. East Boulder, Foothills, and Valmont dog parks are free and open dawn to dusk.

Which Boulder dog parks have small-dog areas?

All three fenced dog parks: Foothills (~0.5 ac), East Boulder (~0.45 ac), and Valmont (~0.25 ac).

Can my dog swim?

East Boulder Dog Park has limited access to a small lake. Valmont has a seasonal water spigot for drinking.

Do I need a Voice and Sight Tag?

Only for the Howard Heuston off-leash area (and Boulder’s other Voice and Sight areas). The three fenced dog parks don’t require one.

Do leash laws apply on the way to the dog park?

Yes. Boulder leash laws apply as you approach or leave any dog park, including in the parking lot.
