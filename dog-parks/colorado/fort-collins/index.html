Best Dog Parks in Fort Collins, CO

An amenity-first look at Fort Collins’ four City dog parks — fencing, hours, parking, and which amenities the City actually confirms.

The City of Fort Collins has four dog parks, each inside a larger park: Fossil Creek (southeast), Soft Gold (north), Spring Canyon (west, near the foothills), and Twin Silo (southeast, the newest community park).

City rules: dogs may be off leash only inside fenced, signed dog park areas; everywhere else in parks they must be leashed. Dogs need a City license and rabies vaccination, and owners must pick up waste. All City parks are open 5 a.m.–11 p.m. daily.

City pages confirm each dog park exists but don’t list water or small-dog areas, so those are marked unknown here (a local guide’s reports are noted where useful). See sources.md.

Problems at the dog park? Call Animal Control For barking or aggressive dogs at a dog park, the City says to call Animal Control at 970-226-3647 or the City Nuisance Hotline at 970-416-2220. Parks Division: 970-221-6660 (after-hours 970-219-3450).

FAQ — Fort Collins dog parks

Where are the dog parks in Fort Collins?

Fossil Creek, Soft Gold, Spring Canyon, and Twin Silo parks, per the City Parks FAQ.

Can my dog be off leash in other Fort Collins parks?

No. Dogs may be off leash only inside fenced, signed dog park areas. Leash them everywhere else.

What are the hours?

All City parks, dog parks included, are open 5 a.m.–11 p.m. daily. Entering a park between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m. is prohibited.

What does my dog need?

A City license and current rabies vaccination. Owners must remove waste.

Who do I call about an aggressive dog?

Animal Control at 970-226-3647 or the City Nuisance Hotline at 970-416-2220.

Is there water and a small-dog area?

City pages don’t say. A local guide reports water and small-dog areas at Fossil Creek, Spring Canyon, and Twin Silo — bring a bowl just in case.
