# Sources — Lakewood, CO dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Lakewood city hub (Denver Metro). Facilities and amenity flags come from official city (or operating-agency) pages; gaps are marked **unknown**. Secondary sources are used only to cross-check and are labeled on the page.

## Primary (official)

- Forsberg-Iron Spring Park (dog park, restrooms, Hayden Park trailhead):  
  https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/Community-Resources/Parks-Forestry-and-Open-Space/A-to-Z-Park-List/Forsberg-Iron-Spring-Park
- Mountair Park (2024 dog park on old ballfield + fencing):  
  https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/Community-Resources/Parks-Forestry-and-Open-Space/A-to-Z-Park-List/Mountair-Park
- Wright Park (opened 2026, small neighborhood dog park):  
  https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/Community-Resources/Parks-Forestry-and-Open-Space/A-to-Z-Park-List/Wright-Park
- A–Z Park List (feature filter includes “Dog Park - Off-leash”):  
  https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/Community-Resources/Parks-Forestry-and-Open-Space/A-to-Z-Park-List
- Bear Creek Lake Park (leash required):  
  https://www.lakewoodco.gov/Local-Government/Departments/Community-Resources/Parks-Forestry-and-Open-Space/A-to-Z-Park-List/Bear-Creek-Lake-Park
