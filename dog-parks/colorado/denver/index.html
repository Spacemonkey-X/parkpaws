Best Dog Parks in Denver, CO

An amenity-first look at every Denver Parks & Recreation dog park — fence type, acreage, drinking water, separate play areas, hours, and the City’s posted rules.

Denver Parks & Recreation (DPR) lists 17 off-leash dog parks, from the 4.1-acre Lowry Dog Park to tiny downtown runs like Little Boxcar (0.1 acre). Two of them — Paco Sanchez and Skyline Park — are labeled pop-up sites, and Zeckendorf Plaza is a pilot that moved from Quality Hill Park in May 2023.

Fence type, size, surfacing, drinking fountains, and whether a park has separate high- and low-energy areas come from the City’s 2019 Dog Park Master Plan Technical Update inventory. Parks built after 2019 (Sanderson Gulch, plus the pop-ups and Zeckendorf) are not in that inventory, so we mark their amenities unknown.

Denver rules are stricter than most cities: no more than two dogs per person, dogs must be spayed/neutered or have a Denver Intact Permit, current rabies tag and Denver dog license are required, and kids 10 and under are not allowed inside. See sources.md.

Only Railyard is lighted — everything else closes at sundown Railyard Dog Park (19th & Little Raven) is Denver’s only lighted dog park and is open 5 a.m.–11 p.m. All other Denver dog parks are open sunrise to sundown, and dogs are not allowed in a dark, unlighted dog park. Check the green/yellow/red condition sign at the gate — red means the park is closed. Report problems to 3-1-1 (720-913-1311 outside Denver).

FAQ — Denver dog parks

How many dog parks does Denver have?

Denver Parks & Recreation lists 17 off-leash dog parks, including two pop-ups (Paco Sanchez and Skyline Park) and the Zeckendorf Plaza pilot. A Central Street dog run along I-25 is in the works.

What are Denver dog park hours?

Unlighted dog parks are open sunrise to sundown, 7 days a week. Railyard, the only lighted dog park, is open 5 a.m.–11 p.m. Dogs aren’t allowed in an unlighted dog park after dark.

How many dogs can I bring?

No more than two dogs per person. The handler must be 16 or older; kids 11–15 need an adult with them, and kids 10 and under aren’t allowed inside.

Does my dog need to be fixed?

Yes, unless you hold a Denver Intact Permit (Denver residents). Dogs also need a current rabies tag on the collar or harness and a Denver dog license. Female dogs in heat and puppies too young for rabies shots aren’t allowed.

Can I bring food or treats?

Dog treats are allowed; human food is not. Fill any holes your dog digs and pick up waste right away — bag dispensers are at every Denver dog park.

What does the colored sign at the gate mean?

Green means good condition, yellow is a warning that users need to clean up or it will close, and red means the park is closed until further notice.

Which Denver dog park is biggest?

Lowry Dog Park (4.1 acres) followed by Greenway (2.9) and Kennedy (2.2), per the City’s 2019 inventory.
