Best Dog Parks in Corona, CA

An amenity-first look at Corona’s designated dog park areas — City rules first, with unconfirmed amenities clearly labeled.

Corona Animal Services lists two designated dog park areas: Butterfield Park (1886 Butterfield Dr) and Griffin Park (420 Somerset Cir). The City publishes detailed rules but few amenity details, so most amenity fields below are marked unknown.

City dog-park rules: no more than two dogs per person; dogs over 4 months and vaccinated for rabies and parvo (carry records); dogs visible and under voice control; posted hours only; no sick, in-heat, or aggressive dogs; quiet excessive barking; children 14 and under supervised by someone 18+; pick up and dispose of waste.

FAQ — Corona dog parks

How many dogs can I bring to a Corona dog park?

No more than two dogs per person, per City rules.

What vaccinations are required?

Dogs must be over 4 months old and vaccinated for rabies and parvo, and you must be able to show records on request.

What are the hours?

Dogs are allowed only during posted hours. The City does not list hours online, so check the sign at each park.

Can kids come into the dog park?

Yes, but children 14 and under must be with an adult 18 or older and must not run, shout, or excite the dogs.

Are there small-dog areas?

The City does not say. Local listings report a small-dog yard at Griffin; confirm on arrival.

What should I bring?

Leash (required outside the dog area), waste bags, vaccination records, and fresh water with a bowl — the City does not confirm water inside either dog area.
