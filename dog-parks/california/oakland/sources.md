# Sources — Oakland, CA dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Oakland city hub. Inventory is the City of Oakland official dog-friendly parks list (5 fenced + 4 off-leash). Chosen over Berkeley because Oakland publishes a complete off-leash set on one .gov page.
