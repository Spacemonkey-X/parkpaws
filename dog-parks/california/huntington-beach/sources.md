# Sources — Huntington Beach, CA dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Huntington Beach city hub (Orange County). Facilities and amenity flags come from official city (or operating-agency) pages; gaps are marked **unknown**. Secondary sources are used only to cross-check or are labeled on the page.
