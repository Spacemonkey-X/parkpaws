# Sources — San Francisco, CA dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the San Francisco city hub. Parks are taken from the SF Recreation and Parks Dog Play Areas inventory; amenity gaps are marked **unknown**.

## Primary (City / SF Rec & Park)

- Dog Play Areas (36 designated sites, rules, Health Code citations):  
  https://www.sfrecpark.org/457/Dog-Play-Areas
- Mission Bay Dog Parks (Berry Street & Channel Street amenities):  
  https://sfrecpark.org/1725/Mission-Bay-Dog-Parks
- SF Park Finder PDF (addresses / amenity chips for many DPAs):  
  https://www.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/36_SFRecreationandParks_SFParkFinder.pdf
- San Francisco Health Code Sec. 40, 41.12, 41.15, 41.18 (leash, waste, license, rabies) — linked from Rec & Park Dog Play Areas page

## Secondary (amenity cross-check only)

- Bay Area Dog SF neighborhood DPA guide (fencing / small-dog notes — not a substitute for posted signs):  
  https://www.bayarea.dog/en/guides/sf-neighborhood-dog-parks-dpa-guide
