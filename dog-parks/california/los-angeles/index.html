Best Dog Parks in Los Angeles, CA

An amenity-first look at the City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks’ official off-leash dog parks — fencing, water, small-dog space, hours, and posted LAMC rules.

Los Angeles Recreation and Parks (RAP) lists 13 official off-leash dog parks inside city limits — from Westminster a few blocks from Venice Beach to Sepulveda Basin in the San Fernando Valley. Neighboring cities (Santa Monica, Culver City, Pasadena, Long Beach, etc.) run their own inventories and are not covered on this hub.

Typical hours are sunrise to sunset, with weekly morning maintenance closures that vary by park (for example Griffith and Whitnall close Tuesday mornings; Sepulveda Basin and Laurel Canyon close Friday mornings — confirm on each RAP park page). Dogs must be licensed, vaccinated, and spayed/neutered (LAMC citations on the city rules board). No food or treats inside dog parks.

Below we cover all 13 parks on the official RAP list with verified amenity fields where city or RAP partnership materials support them. Unknowns are labeled rather than guessed — see sources.md.

SoCal park hazards Summer heat (especially in the Valley), foxtails in dry grass, coyotes at dawn/dusk near hillside parks, and rattlesnakes on canyon trails all matter more in LA than in many US cities. Bring fresh water, check paws/ears after grassy parks, and keep small dogs close on open trails like Runyon Canyon.

Sepulveda Basin Off-Leash Dog Park Fenced

17550 Victory Blvd · Encino / San Fernando Valley

  • Fenced Yes — ~6.5-acre enclosed park
  • Water Drinking fountains / stations reported (city sponsorship notes + local guides)
  • Small-dog area Yes — large separate area for small or timid dogs; on-leash picnic area adjacent
  • Hours Sunrise–sunset; closed Fridays 6–11 a.m. for maintenance (city park page)

Largest City of LA enclosed dog park. Valley heat is real in summer — go early and bring extra water.

Laurel Canyon Dog Park Fenced

8260 Mulholland Dr · Hollywood Hills / Studio City

  • Fenced Yes — ~3 acres (city’s original dog park)
  • Water On-site fountains; city tourism notes washing station after 2023 renovation
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate small/timid section
  • Hours Sunrise–sunset; closed Fridays 6–11 a.m. for maintenance

Ridge-top favorite between Hollywood and Studio City. Expect after-work crowds; limited shade compared with basin parks.

Griffith Park Dog Park Fenced

North Zoo Dr (north end of John Ferraro Soccer Field) · Griffith Park

  • Fenced Yes — ~1.6 acres
  • Water Unknown / not verified on city page — bring your own
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate area for small or timid dogs (city sponsorship notes)
  • Hours Sunrise–sunset; closed Tuesdays 6–10 a.m. for maintenance

Near the Zoo / 134–5 freeway side of Griffith — not near the Observatory. Watch for coyotes and rattlesnakes on surrounding hillside trails.

Valley option under power-line corridor; easy freeway access. Little natural shade — plan for heat.

Runyon Canyon Dog Park Open hillside

2000 N Fuller Avenue · Hollywood Hills

  • Fenced No — hillside trails / open off-leash areas (~90 acres cited by city tourism), not a gated neighborhood run

On the official RAP dog-parks list, but functions as a trail park shared with hikers. Heat, foxtails, coyotes, and rattlesnakes are real risks — strong recall only; keep small dogs close at dawn/dusk.

FAQ — Los Angeles dog parks

Do dogs need to be on a leash in LA parks?

Yes, except inside a designated RAP off-leash dog park. City rules require dogs leashed in parking areas (LAMC 53.06.2). Off-leash only within the posted dog-park boundary.

What are the typical hours?

Most City of LA dog parks are open sunrise to sunset. Individual parks post weekly maintenance closures (often a weekday morning window). Check the specific RAP dog-park page and on-site signs before you go.

Do dogs need to be spayed/neutered or licensed?

Yes. Posted RAP rules require dogs over four months to be fully vaccinated and licensed, wearing collars and tags, and spayed or neutered (LAMC 53.15.2 among others). Puppies under four months and females in heat are not allowed.

Can I bring treats or food?

No. The city posts that absolutely no food or treats are allowed inside dog parks because food can lead to aggression. Bring water for yourself; use a bowl for your dog without sharing food with other dogs.

Which LA dog park is closest to the beach?

Westminster Dog Park in Venice (1234 Pacific Avenue) is the closest City of LA off-leash park to the Boardwalk. Dogs are not allowed on most Los Angeles County beaches — Rosie’s Dog Beach in Long Beach is the well-known county off-leash beach exception (see our Long Beach hub).

Is Runyon Canyon really a dog park?

Runyon Canyon appears on the official RAP dog-parks list, but it is a hillside trail park with open off-leash areas shared with hikers — not a double-gated neighborhood run. Follow posted boundaries, leash in parking areas, and use strong recall only.
