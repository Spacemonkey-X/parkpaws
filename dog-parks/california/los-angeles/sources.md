# Sources — Los Angeles, CA dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Los Angeles city hub. Only parks on the City of Los Angeles Recreation and Parks official dog-parks inventory are published; amenity gaps are marked **unknown** on the page.

## Primary (City of Los Angeles Recreation and Parks)

- Dog Parks inventory (all 13 parks + posted LAMC rules board):  
  https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/dogparks
- Griffith Park Dog Park (hours / Tuesday maintenance):  
  https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/dogpark/griffith
- Sepulveda Basin Off-Leash Dog Park (hours / Friday maintenance):  
  https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/dogpark/sepulveda
- Laurel Canyon Dog Park (hours / Friday maintenance):  
  https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/dogpark/laurel-canyon
- Whitnall Off-Leash Dog Park (hours / Tuesday maintenance):  
  https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/dogpark/whitnall
- Dog Parks sponsorship / amenity summaries (acreage, small-dog sections, shade):  
  https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/partnerships/pdf/dogparks-sponsorships.pdf
- LA Tourism — Parks neighborhood gems (addresses, small-dog notes, Runyon acreage, openings):  
  https://tourism.lacity.gov/neighborhood-gems/parks
- Park Ranger FAQ (off-leash only in designated dog parks):  
  https://recreation.parks.lacity.gov/ranger/frequently-asked-questions-park-ranger
