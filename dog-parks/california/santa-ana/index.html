Best Dog Parks in Santa Ana, CA

An amenity-first look at Santa Ana’s brand-new City dog parks — size-separated yards, water, and what the City has published so far.

Santa Ana opened its first dog park at Centennial Park on April 4, 2026, and its second at Mabury Park on August 8, 2026 (City of Santa Ana). Both split dogs into small, medium, and large areas.

Because both parks are new, the City has not yet published detailed hours or a dog-park rules page on its park listings. Follow posted signs, keep dogs leashed outside the off-leash areas, and carry a current license — see the City’s dog rules FAQ.

Below are the 2 City dog parks. See sources.md.

Two parks, both opened in 2026 Centennial (west side, 4.5 acres) is the destination park with an agility course and dog-rinse station. Mabury (east side) is a smaller neighborhood park with fenced yards, shade seating, and a hydration station.

Centennial Park Dog Park Small-dog area

SW corner of Centennial Park, 3000 W Edinger Ave, Santa Ana 92704

  • Fenced Three separate off-leash areas (City); fence/gate details not stated in the release
  • Water Dog-rinse station (City); drinking water for dogs not specified — bring a bowl
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate areas for small, medium, and large dogs
  • Hours Not published on City pages yet — follow posted hours

Opened April 4, 2026 on a previously undeveloped 4.5-acre lot. Natural turf irrigated with recycled water, canine agility course, seating, new restrooms, walking path, and nearby parking (466 stalls in Centennial Park).

Mabury Park Dog Park Fenced

Mabury Park, 1801 E Fruit St, Santa Ana 92701

  • Fenced Yes — separate fenced areas (City)
  • Water Yes — hydration station for pets and owners (City)
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate areas for small, medium, and large dogs
  • Hours Not published on City pages yet — follow posted hours

Opened August 8, 2026 on underused City land. Shaded seating, new grass and irrigation, waste-bag dispensers. Street parking only at Mabury Park (City listing).

FAQ — Santa Ana dog parks

How many dog parks does Santa Ana have?

Two City dog parks as of fall 2026: Centennial Park (opened April 2026) and Mabury Park (opened August 2026).

Are there separate areas for small dogs?

Yes. The City says both parks have separate areas for small, medium, and large dogs.

What are the hours?

The City has not posted dog-park hours on its park pages yet. Check the signs at the gate.

Is there water?

Mabury has a hydration station for dogs and owners. Centennial has a dog-rinse station; bring drinking water and a bowl to be safe.

Where do I park?

Centennial Park has large lots off Edinger Ave. Mabury Park has street parking only, per the City listing.
