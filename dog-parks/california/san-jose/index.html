Best Dog Parks in San Jose, CA

An amenity-first look at City of San José dog parks — fencing, water, small-dog pens, hours, and posted rules from the official dog-parks directory.

The City of San José Parks, Recreation and Neighborhood Services department maintains a dedicated Dog Parks facility directory with named enclosed parks across downtown, south, west, and east neighborhoods. We chose San José over Sacramento for this hub because the city page includes per-park amenity writeups in one inventory.

Outside designated dog parks, dogs must stay on leash. City dog-park rules cap handlers at two dogs, require licenses/vaccinations, and ban dogs in heat. General park hours are sunrise to one hour after sunset.

Below we cover 12 city dog parks from that directory. Unknowns are labeled — see sources.md. Hellyer County Park’s off-leash area is noted in the FAQ as a nearby county option.

Two-dog limit San José’s posted dog-park rules allow no more than two dogs per person and require a guardian with any children inside the fence. Full list: sanjoseca.gov dog parks.

FAQ — San Jose dog parks

Do dogs need to be on a leash in San José parks?

Yes, except inside designated dog parks. City guidance and San Jose Code 13.44.020 apply: dogs off-leash only where posted. Park rules also prohibit off-leash dogs outside those areas.

What are the dog-park rules?

Per the city dog-parks page: accompany dogs at all times; no more than two dogs per person; children only with a guardian; pick up waste; dogs need license proof and vaccinations; no dogs in heat; remove aggressive dogs.

What are typical hours?

City parks generally open at sunrise and close one hour after sunset unless a site posts otherwise. Check notices for temporary closures (for example turf work at Raleigh Linear).

Is Hellyer County Park included?

Santa Clara County’s Hellyer Park maintains a separate fenced off-leash area (fee for vehicles; closed Wednesdays per city note). It appears on San José’s dog-parks page as an “other off-leash location” but is not a City of San José park — we kept this hub to the 12 city sites.

What should I bring?

Leash, waste bags, water and a bowl (Miyuki and Ryland have no fountains). Summer turf and DG get hot — go early. Tick/flea prevention for creek-adjacent parks (Tully / Coyote Creek).
