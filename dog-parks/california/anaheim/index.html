Best Dog Parks in Anaheim, CA

An amenity-first look at City of Anaheim dog parks — fencing, water, small-dog space, hours, and what the City actually documents.

The City of Anaheim’s facility directory lists a Dog Park feature at four parks: Olive Hills (Anaheim Hills), La Palma (central), Maxwell (west), and Jacaranda (Platinum Triangle). Detail varies a lot by page — Olive Hills and La Palma are well documented; Maxwell and Jacaranda pages only confirm that a dog park exists.

Anaheim parks generally run 5 a.m.–10:30 p.m. unless posted otherwise; Olive Hills is posted dawn to dusk. Keep dogs leashed outside the dog-park gates and follow posted rules at each entrance.

Below we highlight 4 City dog parks, marking fields the City does not confirm as unknown. See sources.md.

Best-documented picks: Olive Hills and La Palma Both have separate small- and large-dog areas per the City. La Palma (opened 2015) adds watering stations, double-gate entries, and late park hours; Olive Hills (2016) adds agility gear, a perimeter path, and the bronze K-9 “Vigilance” monument.

Olive Hills Dog Park Small-dog area

700 S Nohl Canyon Rd, Anaheim 92807 · Anaheim Hills, next to Olive Hills tennis courts

  • Fenced Enclosed dog-use areas implied; perimeter fence details not stated on City page
  • Water Unknown on City page — local guides report fountains; bring your own
  • Small-dog area Yes — separated small and large dog use areas (City)
  • Hours Dawn to dusk, every day (City)

Opened August 2016. Perimeter walking/jogging path, patches of artificial turf, agility equipment, benches, native plants, and the bronze K-9 Monument “Vigilance” honoring Anaheim PD K-9 officers. Restrooms, tennis and pickleball at the park.

La Palma Dog Park Fenced

La Palma Park, 1151 N. La Palma Pkwy · NW corner of La Palma Ave & Anaheim Blvd

  • Fenced Yes — 6-ft perimeter fence divided by an interior fence; two entrances with double gates (City opening release)
  • Water Yes — watering stations (City opening release)
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate small and large dog areas
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10:30 p.m. daily (La Palma Park hours), unless posted

Opened March 7, 2015 in historic La Palma Park (1939). Shade trees, benches, ADA accessible. Parking on the north side along La Palma Park Way. Park also has restrooms.

Maxwell Park Dog Park Dog park

Maxwell Park, 2655 W Orange Ave, Anaheim 92804 · West Anaheim

  • Fenced Unknown — City lists a Dog Park feature but no fencing detail
  • Water Unknown — bring your own
  • Small-dog area Unknown — confirm posted signs
  • Hours 5 a.m. – 10:30 p.m. daily (Maxwell Park hours), unless posted

22-acre park with restrooms, lighted fields, and a new inclusive playground. The dog park is confirmed on the City facility page; amenity details are thin, so unknowns are labeled.

Jacaranda Park Dog Park Dog park

1955 S. Jacaranda St, Anaheim 92805 · Platinum Triangle

  • Fenced Unknown — City lists a Dog Park feature but no fencing detail
  • Water Unknown on City page — local guides report a fountain that may be off in winter
  • Small-dog area Unknown on City page — local guides report small/large sections
  • Hours Not listed on City page; third-party listings show 7 a.m.–7 p.m. — confirm posted

1.1-acre park completed November 2019 in the Jefferson Stadium Park community; the City calls it the area’s first dog park. Also has bocce, basketball, fitness equipment, and picnic areas.

FAQ — Anaheim dog parks

How many dog parks does Anaheim have?

The City facility directory lists a dog park at four parks: Olive Hills, La Palma, Maxwell, and Jacaranda.

Which Anaheim dog park is best for small dogs?

Olive Hills and La Palma both have separate small- and large-dog areas per the City. Maxwell and Jacaranda pages do not confirm size separation.

What are typical hours?

Most Anaheim parks list 5 a.m.–10:30 p.m. (La Palma, Maxwell). Olive Hills is dawn to dusk. Jacaranda’s hours are not on the City page — check the posted sign.

Is there water at the parks?

La Palma has watering stations per the City. For the others the City does not say — bring a bowl and water, especially in summer.

Where do I park for La Palma Dog Park?

On the north side of La Palma Park along La Palma Park Way, per the City’s opening release.

What is the K-9 monument at Olive Hills?

A bronze monument called “Vigilance” honoring Anaheim Police Department K-9 officers, dedicated with the dog park in August 2016.
