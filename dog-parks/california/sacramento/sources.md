## Notes

- All 15 names and the numbered rules are from the Off-Leash Dog Parks page. The Specialty Parks page’s “14” count is noted on the hub rather than silently ignored.
- Fencing is marked Yes for every listing because the official overview says all YPCE dog parks are fenced with a double-gate system.
- Water and small-dog areas are marked Yes only when a park page or the list’s per-park notes specifically mention them. The overview’s general “drinking fountains for dogs” sentence is mentioned in the intro, not applied as a per-card Yes.
- Hours are marked Unknown: the official dog-parks page does not publish opening or closing times.
- Olympians Park appears on the current official list at 350 Crate Avenue; its directory page still describes a dog park among “future” amenities. That wording is disclosed on the card.
- University Park’s directory page lists playgrounds and picnic areas but does not separately itemize the dog park; the listing is included because the official dog-parks page names University Avenue Dog Park at 1900 University Avenue.
- Listing images are puppy placeholders (see images/PHOTO-CREDITS.md), not photos of these parks.
