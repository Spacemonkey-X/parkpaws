Best Dog Parks in Irvine, CA

An amenity-first look at Irvine’s official off-leash dog park — fencing, water, small-dog space, hours, and posted City of Irvine rules.

Irvine has one official City off-leash dog park: Central Bark on Oak Canyon, next to the Irvine Animal Care Center. Opened in 2000, it covers almost three acres of open field with a main yard and a separate fenced area for small or frail dogs, a dog watering facility, covered seating, lighting, and handicap access (City of Irvine).

Posted Central Bark rules: each dog under control of an adult 18+, no more than three dogs per adult, collar and current license, vaccinations current, leash on entry/exit, no choke, prong, or spike collars, no food of any kind, no sports equipment, no puppies under 4 months or dogs in heat, and no professional trainers giving instruction.

Because Irvine’s inventory is a single park, this hub goes deep on that one site and flags what is not off-leash (Quail Hill and other open-space trails) and what is only planned (a Great Park dog park). See sources.md.

Only one official off-leash park — for now Central Bark is Irvine’s only City off-leash dog park. Quail Hill and other Irvine open-space trails are leashed-dog areas, not off-leash parks. In April 2025 the Great Park Board directed staff to add a dog park in the Great Park’s Heart of the Park district; it is still in planning and not open.

  • Fenced Yes — almost 3 acres; main yard plus a separate fenced small/frail-dog area (City)
  • Water Yes — watering facility for dogs (City)

FAQ — Irvine dog parks

Does Irvine have more than one off-leash dog park?

Not today. Central Bark is the City’s only official off-leash dog park. A Great Park dog park was added to the Great Park Framework Plan in 2025, but it has no announced opening date.

Is Quail Hill off-leash?

No. Treat Quail Hill and other Irvine open-space trails as leash-required. Off-leash play is only allowed inside designated areas like Central Bark.

When is Central Bark closed?

It is closed for maintenance on Wednesdays (the City page currently says the second Wednesday of each month) and may close after rain. Call 949-724-MUDD or check the City Field Conditions page before driving over.

How many dogs can I bring?

Up to three dogs per adult guardian, per the posted Central Bark rules. Each dog needs a collar with a current license and up-to-date vaccinations.

Can I bring treats, balls, or my trainer?

No food of any kind (human or dog), no sports equipment, and professional trainers may not use the park for instruction. Choke, prong, and spike collars must come off inside the fenced areas.

Is there a small-dog area?

Yes — a separate fenced yard for small or frail dogs. Large breeds found there will be asked to leave, and the City advises against bringing small dogs into the large-dog yard.
