# Sources — Mesa, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Mesa city hub on 2026-11-09 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked **Unknown** on the page.

## Primary — City of Mesa Parks, Recreation & Community Facilities

- Dog Parks / Off-Leash Areas overview ("operates three off-leash areas": Countryside, Quail Run, Eastmark Great Park; temporary dog park notes): https://www.mesaparks.com/parks-facilities/dog-parks
- ParkFinder GIS, Off-Leash Dog Area layer (returned Quail Run Park and Eastmark Great Park points on 2026-11-09): https://gis.mesaaz.gov/mesaaz/rest/services/Parks/ParkFinder/MapServer/10
- Park Rules (6 ft physical leash outside the double-fenced dog park area; waste pickup; no dogs in playgrounds; no electronic leashes): https://www.mesaaz.gov/Activities-Culture/Parks-Recreation-and-Community-Facilities/Park-Rules
- Animal Control FAQ (Maricopa County rabies vaccination + annual license): https://www.mesaaz.gov/Resident-Resources/Animal-Control

Fenced: Mesa Park Rules refer to the "double fenced safety and security area" of designated dog parks. Small-dog area: Yes only for Eastmark Great Park ("Two off leash dog areas – one for active dogs and one for timid dogs"). Restrooms are listed in each park's Features list (park-level, not necessarily at the dog area). Water and dog-area shade are not listed on any city page and are marked Unknown. Quail Run's "Temporary Closure" notice (storm damage and flooding, closed until further notice) was live on 2026-11-09.

Not listed: past temporary dog parks (e.g., Gene Autry Park, used during Quail Run turf work per a 2020 City of Mesa Nextdoor post https://nextdoor.com/agency-post/az/mesa/city-of-mesa/temporary-dog-park-149518022/) are not permanent and are excluded.
