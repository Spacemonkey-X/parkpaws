Best Dog Parks in Mesa, AZ

An amenity-first look at Mesa’s three city off-leash dog parks: small-dog yards, restrooms, weekly maintenance days, and which park is closed right now.

The City of Mesa Parks, Recreation & Community Facilities department says it operates three off-leash dog areas: Eastmark Great Park, Countryside Park, and Quail Run Park (Mesa dog parks). Mesa’s park rules describe designated dog parks as double-fenced areas.

Each dog park closes one weekday for maintenance (Countryside on Tuesdays, Eastmark on Wednesdays, Quail Run on Thursdays) and every day from noon to 1 p.m. for cleaning. Parks are open sunrise to 10 p.m. Plan around these windows.

Amenities come from each park’s city page. Those pages list features for the whole park, so restrooms are marked Yes at the park level. Water and shade inside the dog yards are not listed, so we mark them Unknown. Eastmark is the only park whose page names a separate area for timid dogs.

Rules from the city: dogs must be on a physical leash no longer than 6 feet anywhere outside the double-fenced area, including the walk in and out. Electronic leashes don’t count. Remove an aggressive dog right away, pick up waste, and keep dogs out of playgrounds. Maricopa County requires every dog to have a current rabies vaccination and an annual license.

Quail Run is temporarily closed As of Nov. 9, 2026, the city’s Quail Run Park page says the park is closed until further notice after storm damage and flooding. Until it reopens, use Eastmark Great Park or Countryside Park.

FAQ — Mesa dog parks

What is the best dog park in Mesa?

Based on the amenities the city lists, Eastmark Great Park in southeast Mesa comes out ahead. It has two off-leash areas, one for active dogs and one for timid dogs, and the park has restrooms, a fishing lake, and a splash pad. Countryside Park on Southern Ave. is the central option. Quail Run is closed until further notice.

How many dog parks does Mesa have?

The city says it operates three off-leash areas: Eastmark Great Park, Countryside Park, and Quail Run Park. In past years, the city opened temporary dog parks (for example at Gene Autry Park) during turf work. Those are not permanent parks, so they are not listed here.

Which Mesa dog park has a small-dog area?

Eastmark Great Park is the only Mesa dog park whose city page lists a separate area, which the city calls an area for timid dogs. The Countryside and Quail Run pages do not mention a separate small-dog yard, so we list them as Unknown.

When are Mesa dog parks closed?

Every Mesa dog park closes daily from noon to 1 p.m. for cleaning, plus one weekday for maintenance: Countryside on Tuesdays, Eastmark Great Park on Wednesdays, and Quail Run on Thursdays. The parks are otherwise open sunrise to 10 p.m. Quail Run Park is currently closed until further notice.

Do I need a dog license to use Mesa dog parks?

Yes. The City of Mesa notes that all dogs in Maricopa County must have a current rabies vaccination and a license, which you renew every year through Maricopa County Animal Care and Control.

What should I bring?

Bring a physical leash of 6 feet or less for the walk in, waste bags, and your own water and bowl, since the city pages don’t list water in the dog yards. In summer, go early or late, when the turf and paths are cooler.
