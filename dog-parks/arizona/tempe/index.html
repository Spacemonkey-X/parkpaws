Best Dog Parks in Tempe, AZ

An amenity-first look at all seven of Tempe’s free off-leash dog parks: the new Kiwanis Dog Park with its small-dog pod, late-night hours, weekly maintenance closures, and which amenities the city actually lists.

The City of Tempe runs seven free dog parks: Clark, Creamery, Jaycee, Kiwanis, Mitchell, Papago, and the Tempe Sports Complex (City of Tempe dog parks). Dogs run off leash inside each enclosure, and Tempe’s rules require the handler to stay inside with them.

Kiwanis Dog Park is the standout. It opened in 2025 with four separately gated pods (one for small dogs, one with agility equipment), a hydrant water spray for dogs, and two shade structures. The Tempe Sports Complex dog park covers 2 acres, and Papago Park keeps the longest hours, 5 a.m. to midnight.

For the other parks, the city lists park-wide amenities rather than dog-run details. Where water, shade, or a small-dog area isn’t stated for the dog park itself, we mark it “Not stated” instead of guessing.

Tempe’s rules: dogs must be leashed coming in and going out, licensed, vaccinated, and wearing a visible current license. No more than 3 dogs per handler. No female dogs in heat, no aggressive dogs, and no food in the dog run. Children under 12 need an adult. Pick up after your dog (it’s the law in Tempe) and fill any holes your dog digs.

Plan around maintenance closures Clark closes Tuesdays 9:30–11 a.m. Papago is under maintenance Wednesdays 5 a.m.–12:30 p.m., Mitchell is closed Wednesdays 6 a.m.–1 p.m., and the Tempe Sports Complex is closed Wednesdays 10 a.m.–12:30 p.m. Kiwanis closes Thursdays 10 a.m.–12:30 p.m. The city doesn’t list a weekly closure for Jaycee or Creamery. Check the city dog park listing before you go.

Kiwanis Dog Park Newest + small-dog pod

Kiwanis Park, Mill Ave. & All America Way, 85283, Tempe, AZ · South Tempe · north end of Kiwanis Park near Ash Ave. and Baseline Rd. · City of Tempe Parks & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — decorative steel fence; four separate pods with their own entrances
  • Water Yes — hydrant water spray feature for dogs
  • Shade Yes — two shade structures plus new shade trees
  • Small-dog area Yes — one of the four pods is for small dogs
  • Restrooms Restrooms listed for Kiwanis Park; the north restroom building was closed for construction starting Dec. 2025 (est. completion summer/fall 2026) with temporary portable toilets
  • Hours Daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m. per the city’s project page (the park listing says 11 p.m.); closed Thursdays 10 a.m.–12:30 p.m. for maintenance

Tempe’s newest dog park opened in 2025 on the north side of 125-acre Kiwanis Park. Four pods include one for small dogs and one with dog agility equipment, plus lighting, benches, accessible parking along Ash Avenue, and a connection to the Western Canal path. The city says the north-end playground and restroom construction doesn’t affect the dog park. City page.

FAQ — Tempe dog parks

What is the best dog park in Tempe?

Kiwanis Dog Park, on the north side of Kiwanis Park near Ash Avenue and Baseline Road. It’s the newest, opened in 2025, and the only Tempe dog park where the city lists a small-dog pod, agility equipment, a water spray feature, and shade structures.

How many dog parks does Tempe have?

Seven free city dog parks: Clark, Creamery, Jaycee, Kiwanis, Mitchell, Papago, and the Tempe Sports Complex.

Is there a small-dog area at Tempe dog parks?

Yes, at Kiwanis Dog Park, where one of the four pods is for small dogs. The city doesn’t list a small-dog area for its other six dog parks.

Which Tempe dog park is open latest?

Papago, Mitchell, Jaycee, and Creamery are listed as open until midnight. Papago also opens earliest, at 5 a.m. The Tempe Sports Complex dog park stays open until 11 p.m. from May through October.

When are Tempe dog parks closed for maintenance?

Clark: Tuesdays 9:30–11 a.m. Papago: Wednesdays 5 a.m.–12:30 p.m. Mitchell: Wednesdays 6 a.m.–1 p.m. Tempe Sports Complex: Wednesdays 10 a.m.–12:30 p.m. Kiwanis: Thursdays 10 a.m.–12:30 p.m. The city doesn’t list a weekly closure for Jaycee or Creamery.

How many dogs can I bring to a Tempe dog park?

Up to three dogs per owner or handler. Dogs must be licensed, vaccinated, and wearing a visible, current license.
