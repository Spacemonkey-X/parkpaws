# Sources — Tempe, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Tempe city hub on 2026-11-13 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked "Not stated" on the page.

- Dog Parks (seven free dog parks; 14 dog park rules): https://www.tempe.gov/government/community-services/parks/dog-parks
- Dog Parks Listing (addresses, park hours, park-wide amenity lists): https://www.tempe.gov/government/community-services/parks/dog-parks-listing
