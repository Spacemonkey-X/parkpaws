Best Dog Parks in Chandler, AZ

An amenity-first look at Chandler’s four fenced city dog parks: drinking fountains, agility obstacles, the one small-dog section, restrooms, and each park’s weekly maintenance closure.

The City of Chandler lists four dog parks: Snedigar Sportsplex, Paseo Vista Recreation Area, Shawnee Park, and Nozomi Park (Chandler dog parks). Each one is surrounded by a 6-foot chain link fence with one main entrance.

The city says its dog parks have benches, pooper-scoopers, doggy drinking fountains, plenty of parking, and agility obstacles: a see-saw, balance beam, tunnel, and hurdles. Snedigar is the only one with a fake fire hydrant.

Most parks are open 6 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. and close one morning a week (6 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.) for maintenance: Nozomi on Mondays, Snedigar on Wednesdays, and Paseo Vista on Thursdays. Shawnee keeps shorter hours. It closes at 8 p.m., opens at 9 a.m. on Sundays, and is closed all day Tuesday.

Rules from the city ordinance: keep dogs leashed until they are inside the fence and carry a leash for each dog. Dogs must be licensed and vaccinated, puppies must be older than 4 months, and dogs in season are not allowed. The limit is three dogs per owner, no children under 12 may enter, and food and smoking are not allowed inside the fence. Shade inside the dog yards isn’t listed, so we mark it Unknown.

Heads-up on current closures As of Nov. 10, 2026, the city says the small section at Snedigar is closed while its fence is rebuilt (the large section is open), and Nozomi Park’s restrooms are closed.

FAQ — Chandler dog parks

What is the best dog park in Chandler?

Based on what the city lists, Snedigar Sportsplex in south Chandler is the most complete. It is the only park with a separate small section (closed for now during fence repairs), it has a grass field and obstacle course, and the Sportsplex has restrooms. Paseo Vista is a strong pick if you want to add a walk on its two trails.

How many dog parks does Chandler have?

The city lists four: Snedigar Sportsplex, Paseo Vista Recreation Area, Shawnee Park, and Nozomi Park (formerly West Chandler Park). All four are fenced, have drinking fountains for dogs, and have agility obstacles.

Do Chandler dog parks have water?

Yes. The city’s dog parks page lists doggy drinking fountains as a standard amenity. A collapsible bowl still helps on hot days or if a fountain is out of service.

Which Chandler dog park has a small-dog area?

Snedigar is the only park with a separate small section on the city pages, and it is closed while the fence is rebuilt. The other three pages don’t mention a small-dog yard, so we list them as Unknown.

When are Chandler dog parks closed?

Nozomi closes Mondays, Snedigar Wednesdays, and Paseo Vista Thursdays, each from 6 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Shawnee is closed all day Tuesday. If one park is closed for maintenance, another one is open that day.

Can kids come into Chandler dog parks?

No children under 12 are allowed inside the dog parks, and the agility equipment is for dogs only.
