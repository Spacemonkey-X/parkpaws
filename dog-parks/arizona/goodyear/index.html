Best Dog Parks in Goodyear, AZ

An amenity-first look at Goodyear’s two city dog parks: which one is lighted, which one splits dogs by energy level, where to find water and restrooms, and the hours and park rules posted by the City of Goodyear.

If you’re searching “dog park near me” in Goodyear, the City of Goodyear lists two places to let your dog off leash: Roscoe Dog Park, a dedicated 6.5-acre specialty park on Roeser Road in south Goodyear, and the dog park inside Goodyear Community Park on Litchfield Road north of I-10 (City of Goodyear specialty parks). Both follow the city’s park hours of 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.

Where the city doesn’t state an amenity, we mark it “Not stated” instead of guessing. Dog parks inside HOA or private communities aren’t open to the public, so they aren’t listed here.

Goodyear’s general park rules: dogs must be leashed outside designated dog areas, and handlers must pick up pet waste. All Goodyear parks are smoke- and vape-free, glass containers aren’t allowed, and pets (other than service animals) stay out of playgrounds and splash pads (City of Goodyear park rules). Follow any additional rules posted at the dog park gate.

Evening visit? Go to Roscoe. It’s the only Goodyear dog park where the city lists lighting over the dog runs, and its passive-dog area gives shy or older dogs room away from high-energy play. Questions: Goodyear Parks & Recreation, 623-882-7525 (Recreation Office) or 623-640-1604 (Park Ranger).

Roscoe Dog Park 6.5 acres, lighted, active + passive areas

15600 W. Roeser Rd., Goodyear, AZ 85338 · South Goodyear, about 4 miles south of I-10 via Estrella Pkwy. (½ mile south of MC-85, then west on Roeser) · City of Goodyear Parks & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — double-gate entrance
  • Water Yes — water fountains for dogs and people
  • Shade Not stated by the city
  • Small-dog area Not stated. Roscoe splits its yards by energy level (a passive-dog area and an active-dog area), not by size
  • Restrooms Yes — restroom facility
  • Hours Daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m. (city park hours), year-round; the dog run areas are lighted

Goodyear’s dedicated dog park is a 6.5-acre specialty park with separate areas for passive and active dogs, lighting over the dog run areas, water fountains for dogs and people, doggie bags, picnic tables and benches, a double-gate entrance, a restroom building, and an expanded parking lot, per the city. The city’s page has posted temporary maintenance closures in the past, so check it before you drive down. Roscoe is adopted by Petz Place through the city’s Adopt-A-Park program. City of Goodyear: Roscoe Dog Park · City facility directory.

Goodyear Community Park Dog Park Inside the city’s flagship park

Goodyear Community Park, 3151 N. Litchfield Rd., Goodyear, AZ · North of I-10 at the northeast corner of Litchfield and Thomas roads · City of Goodyear Parks & Recreation

  • Fenced Not stated by the city
  • Water Drinking fountains are on the park’s amenity list (not stated whether one is at the dog park)
  • Shade Not stated by the city
  • Small-dog area Not stated by the city
  • Restrooms Yes — restrooms in Goodyear Community Park (location relative to the dog park not stated)
  • Hours Daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m. (city park hours)

The city lists a dog park and doggie bags among Goodyear Community Park’s amenities but doesn’t publish a dog park layout or amenity list. That’s why fencing, shade, and size sections are marked “Not stated” here. The rest of the park has 7 ramadas with BBQ grills, a seasonal splash pad (third Monday in April to third Monday in October, 9 a.m.–8 p.m.), a skate park, tennis, pickleball, basketball, and sand volleyball courts, ball fields, a playground, walking trails, and restrooms. Pets aren’t allowed in the play areas. Recreation Office: 623-882-7525; Park Ranger: 623-640-1604. City of Goodyear: Goodyear Community Park.

FAQ — Goodyear dog parks

What is the best dog park in Goodyear, AZ?

For most dogs, Roscoe Dog Park at 15600 W. Roeser Rd. It’s 6.5 acres with separate passive and active areas, lighted dog runs, water fountains for dogs and people, doggie bags, a double-gate entrance, and a restroom building.

How many dog parks does Goodyear have?

Two city dog parks: Roscoe Dog Park (15600 W. Roeser Rd.) and the dog park in Goodyear Community Park (3151 N. Litchfield Rd.).

What are Goodyear dog park hours?

Goodyear parks are open from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., and the city says Roscoe Dog Park keeps those hours daily, year-round. Dog parks can close temporarily for maintenance, so check the city page before you go.

Does Goodyear have a small-dog park?

The city doesn’t list a small-dog section at either park. Roscoe divides its space into a passive-dog area and an active-dog area instead, which can work well for small or timid dogs.

Is Roscoe Dog Park lighted at night?

Yes. The City of Goodyear lists dog run area lighting at Roscoe, which helps for cooler evening visits before the 10 p.m. close.

Can I smoke or vape at a Goodyear dog park?

No. All City of Goodyear parks are smoke-free, and the city’s park rules prohibit smoking and vaping.
