# Sources — Goodyear, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Goodyear city hub on 2026-11-17 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked "Not stated" on the page.

## Primary — City of Goodyear

- Roscoe Dog Park (15600 W. Roeser Road, Goodyear AZ 85338; open 6 a.m.–10 p.m. daily, year-round; directions ~4 mi south of I-10 on Estrella Pkwy, 1/2 mi south of MC-85, west to Roeser; amenities: area for passive dogs, area for active dogs, dog run area lighting, doggie bags, water fountains for dogs and people, 6.5 acres open park space, picnic table and benches, double-gate entrance, restroom facility, expanded parking lot; smoke-free; adopted by Petz Place). A search-indexed copy also showed a notice "Roscoe Dog Park is closed from April 21 to May 9" (year not stated; not shown on the live page fetched 2026-11-17): https://www.goodyearaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation/parks/specialty-parks/roscoe-dog-park
- Goodyear Community Park (3151 N. Litchfield Rd.; NE corner of Litchfield and Thomas roads, north of I-10; amenities include Dog Park, Doggie bags, Drinking fountains, Restrooms, 7 ramadas with BBQ grills, splash pad [third Monday in April to third Monday in October, 9 a.m.–8 p.m.], skate park, courts, fields, playground, walking trails; "No pets allowed in play areas except for service animals"; Recreation Office 623-882-7525, Park Ranger 623-640-1604): https://www.goodyearaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation/parks/community-parks/goodyear-community-park
- Specialty Parks (lists Goodyear Community Park "and even a dog park" and Roscoe Dog Park with passive and active areas): https://www.goodyearaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation/parks/specialty-parks
- Park Rules (park hours 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; no smoking or vaping; dogs must be on leash and handlers remove pet waste; no glass containers): https://www.goodyearaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation/parks/park-rules
- Facility directory entry, Roscoe Dog Park, 15600 W Roeser Rd Goodyear, AZ 85338: https://www.goodyearaz.gov/Home/Components/FacilityDirectory/FacilityDirectory/46/50?npage=3

Note: goodyearaz.gov returns 403 to curl from the box; pages were read with a fetch tool and search-indexed copies on 2026-11-17.
