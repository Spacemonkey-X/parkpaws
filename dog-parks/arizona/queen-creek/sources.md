# Sources — Queen Creek, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Queen Creek town hub on 2026-11-20 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked "Not stated" on the page.

Candidate selection: Tolleson AZ (planned) and Litchfield Park AZ (backup) were skipped. The City of Tolleson parks pages list only Veterans Park and McNeel Park, whose rules say dogs must be leashed at all times (no designated dog park). The City of Litchfield Park parks page says "The City of Litchfield Park does not have a designated dog park."

## Primary — Town of Queen Creek

- Founders' Park Dog Park ("Hours of Operation January - December: 5:30 a.m. - 10 p.m."; "Park Address: 22407 S. Ellsworth Rd"; "the Town's lighted dog park! The off-leash park consists of four separate grass-play areas, shaded areas, enclosed fencing and a drinking fountains"; reseeding closure of the south small and large sections July 15–29 with north sections open; dog park rules incl. carry a leash per dog, leashed entering/exiting, limit three dogs, dogs older than four months, licensed/vaccinated/visible license, no treats/food, no smoking, no females in heat, children under 12 with an adult, cover holes; Park ranger hotline 480-358-3770): https://www.queencreekaz.gov/community/parks-recreation/queen-creek-parks/founders-park-dog-park
- News release "Founders' Park Dog Park Now Open!" (posted 03/21/2026; shade structures, seating options, separate entrances for large and timid dogs; large-dog entrance on the east side with parking in the Aldecoa lot, south lot recommended for small/timid area; waste stations and signage; open daily 5:30 a.m.–10 p.m.; replaces Pocket Park for Pups, temporary dog park since 2014, now closed): https://www.queencreekaz.gov/Home/Components/News/News/13477/
- Parks list (Founders' Park Dog Park is the only dog park listed; Founders' Park is an 11-acre park across the street from Town Hall; park hours 5:30 a.m.–10 p.m.): https://www.queencreekaz.gov/community/parks-recreation/queen-creek-parks
- Facility Rules and Regulations ("Dogs must be on a leash at all times and must be cleaned up after"): https://www.queencreekaz.gov/community/parks-recreation/facility-regulations
- Town of Queen Creek Public Park Regulations (Section 3(F): leash no greater than six feet and under owner's control, "except within the fenced area of a designated dog park"; pick-up required): https://www.queencreekaz.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/30799/637793284287000000
