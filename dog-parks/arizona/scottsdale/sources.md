# Sources — Scottsdale, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Scottsdale city hub on 2026-11-11 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked **Unknown** on the page.

## Primary — City of Scottsdale

- Dog Parks page (last updated Aug 21, 2026; four parks with addresses, descriptions, Active/Passive areas at Chaparral and Thompson Peak, hours for Chaparral and Thompson Peak, maintenance schedules, rules; intro says shade and dog water fountains at every location): https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/parks/dog-parks
- Thompson Peak Park Dog Park capital project (completed; three fenced and gated cells, shade canopies, benches, water fountains, pet waste stations, footbridge, Hayden Rd parking, new restroom building, art panels by Shachi Kale): https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/capital-improvement/thompson-peak-park-dog-park
