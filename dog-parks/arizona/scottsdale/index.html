Best Dog Parks in Scottsdale, AZ

An amenity-first look at Scottsdale’s four city dog parks: shade, dog water fountains, active and passive dog areas, hours, and the weekly maintenance mornings to plan around.

The City of Scottsdale lists four dog parks: Chaparral, Horizon, Thompson Peak, and Vista del Camino (Scottsdale dog parks). The city says open space, shade, and dog water fountains are at every location, and you are welcome to bring portable chairs and toys.

The two big grass parks, Chaparral (3 acres) and the new Thompson Peak (3.5 acres), are split into three areas: one for Active Dogs, one for Passive Dogs, and a third that rotates closed for turf repair. Both are open 5:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. year-round.

Maintenance mornings run 8 a.m. to noon: Chaparral on Tuesdays and Fridays, and Horizon and Thompson Peak on Mondays and Thursdays. Vista del Camino has no current maintenance schedule. The city page doesn’t publish hours for Horizon or Vista del Camino, and it doesn’t say whether those two are fenced, so we mark those Unknown.

Rules from the city: no more than four dogs per visitor, an adult must come with anyone under 16, dogs must be licensed and vaccinated, puppies under 4 months need their license and shots first, and female dogs in heat are not allowed. No food or alcohol in the exercise area. Outside it, dogs must be on a leash of 6 feet or less.

Heads-up on Horizon after rain The city says Horizon Dog Park sits in a retention basin and is prone to flooding after a storm.

Scottsdale’s newest dog park: 3.5 acres of grass with shade canopies, benches, water fountains, and pet waste stations. The bond-funded project also added a footbridge to the rest of the park, more parking along Hayden Road, and four dog-themed art panels by Scottsdale artist Shachi Kale. City page.

FAQ — Scottsdale dog parks

What is the best dog park in Scottsdale?

The city calls Chaparral its most popular dog park, with 3 acres of grass and separate active and passive areas. In north Scottsdale, the new Thompson Peak Dog Park is larger (3.5 acres), with shade canopies, three fenced and gated cells, and a new restroom building at the park.

How many dog parks does Scottsdale have?

Four city dog parks: Chaparral, Horizon, Thompson Peak, and Vista del Camino. The city page’s intro still says “three,” but it lists all four, including the newest one at Thompson Peak Park.

Do Scottsdale dog parks have shade and water?

Yes. The city says shade and dog water fountains are at every location: shade canopies at Thompson Peak, a large shade structure at Horizon, and shade trees at Vista del Camino. Bring a collapsible bowl for the drive in desert heat.

Is there a small-dog area at Scottsdale dog parks?

Chaparral and Thompson Peak split dogs by temperament, not size: one area for active dogs and one for passive dogs. The city doesn’t list a separate small-dog area at Horizon or Vista del Camino.

When are Scottsdale dog parks closed for maintenance?

From 8 a.m. to noon: Chaparral on Tuesdays and Fridays, and Horizon and Thompson Peak on Mondays and Thursdays. Vista del Camino has no current maintenance schedule.

How many dogs can I bring?

No more than four dogs per visitor, and an adult must accompany anyone under 16.
