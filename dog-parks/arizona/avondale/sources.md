# Sources — Avondale, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Avondale city hub on 2026-11-18 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked "Not stated" on the page.

## Primary — City of Avondale

- Friendship Park (12325 W. McDowell Road, Avondale AZ 85323; park hours Mon–Sun 6:00 AM–10:00 PM; amenities: 1 softball field, 2 tennis courts, 2 Little League baseball fields, 3 playgrounds, 2 basketball courts, splash pad, 9 multi-use fields, Dog Park, walking trails, community fishing pond, Japanese garden, picnic ramadas, water bottle refill stations; Parks & Recreation (623) 333-2400, ActiveAvondale@avondaleAZ.gov): https://avondaleaz.gov/locations/friendship-park/
- Alamar Park (4155 S. El Mirage Road, Avondale AZ 85323; 40-acre community park in the Alamar development; park hours Mon–Sun 6:00 AM–10:00 PM; amenities: multi-use field, amphitheater, pickleball courts, splash pad, tennis courts, basketball courts, picnic ramadas, multiple playgrounds, lake [urban fishing with AZ Game and Fish], fitness court [National Fitness Campaign], Dog park, baseball fields, water bottle refill stations, softball fields): https://avondaleaz.gov/locations/alamar-park/
- Festival Fields Park (101 E. Lower Buckeye Road, Avondale AZ 85323; "underwent a major expansion and renovation in 2020"; park hours Mon–Sun 6:00 AM–10:00 PM; amenities: large group ramada, 2 basketball courts, splash pad, pump track, 4 softball fields, skate park, 2 Little League baseball fields, Dog Park, multi-use fields, community fishing pond, 4 pickleball courts, picnic ramadas, water bottle refill stations): https://avondaleaz.gov/locations/festival-fields-park/
- Community Parks overview ("3 community parks"; "Avondale community parks are open daily from 6:00 am – 10:00 pm"; Alamar 40 acres with "dog parks"; Festival Fields 40+ acres west of the Agua Fria River with "dog park"; Friendship 60+ acres on McDowell Road just north of I-10 with "dog parks"): https://avondaleaz.gov/government/departments/prld/parks/community-parks/
- Park Rules (Friendship, Alamar and Festival Fields community park rules, Avondale City Code 17-5: "Dogs must always remain leashed unless in designated dog park locations"; glass containers prohibited; open-flame portable grills prohibited; no swimming, boating, or toys in the ponds; dispose of litter in receptacles): https://avondaleaz.gov/government/departments/prld/parks/park-rules-2/
