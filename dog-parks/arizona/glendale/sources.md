# Sources — Glendale, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Glendale city hub on 2026-11-14 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked "Not stated" on the page.

## Primary — City of Glendale

- Dog Parks (four off-leash dog parks; locations, amenities, maintenance days, 13 rules): https://www.glendaleaz.gov/Play/Parks-and-Recreation/Facilities-Centers/Special-Facilities/Dog-Parks
  (also served at https://www.glendaleaz.gov/Explore/Parks-and-Recreation/Facilities-Centers/Special-Facilities/Dog-Parks)
- Foothills Park (57th Ave. & Union Hills Dr., 85308; park hours 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; dog park with grass, small-dog areas, shade, agility course; features list): https://www.glendaleaz.gov/Play/Parks-and-Recreation/Parks-Trails/Regional-Parks/Foothills-Park
- Sahuaro Ranch Park (9802 N 59th Ave., 85302; park hours daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; dog park north of the west parking lot, south of the softball complex): https://www.glendaleaz.gov/Explore/Parks-and-Recreation/Parks-Trails/Regional-Parks/Sahuaro-Ranch-Park
- Glendale Heroes Regional Park (83rd Ave. & Bethany Home Rd., 85303; park hours daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; features incl. dog park and restrooms): https://www.glendaleaz.gov/Play/Parks-and-Recreation/Parks-Trails/Regional-Parks/Glendale-Heroes-Regional-Park
- Heroes Regional Park upgrades groundbreaking (May 17, 2024; dog park part of new phase): https://www.glendaleaz.gov/News/Whats-New/Heroes-Regional-Park-Upgrades
