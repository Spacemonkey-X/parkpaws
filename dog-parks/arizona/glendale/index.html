Best Dog Parks in Glendale, AZ

An amenity-first look at Glendale’s four city off-leash dog parks: which ones have small-dog areas, agility equipment, water, and shade, plus the weekly maintenance day for each.

The City of Glendale runs four off-leash dog parks: Foothills Park, Glendale Heroes Park, Northern Horizon Park, and Sahuaro Ranch Park (City of Glendale dog parks). The city lists a small-dog area at all four and water at every one, which makes Glendale one of the easier West Valley cities for owners of small or timid dogs.

Foothills Park is the most complete: two sides that rotate monthly, each with large- and small-dog spaces, grass, shade, water, and benches, plus an agility course on the east side. Sahuaro Ranch also has agility equipment. Glendale Heroes Park has the newest dog park, part of the park’s latest expansion.

Two parks, Foothills and Heroes, rotate between sections month to month. Signs at the gate show which side is open. Where the city doesn’t state an amenity, we mark it “Not stated” instead of guessing.

Glendale’s rules: dogs must be licensed, vaccinated, and wearing ID and permit tags, and leashed coming in and going out. Stay with your dog and carry a visible leash. Up to 3 dogs per visit. No female dogs in heat, no aggressive dogs, and no dog treats or food. Children under 13 need an adult. Pick up after your dog (City Code 27-48A) and repair any digging right away.

Every park has a weekly maintenance day Northern Horizon is closed Mondays, Sahuaro Ranch Tuesdays, Foothills Wednesdays, and Glendale Heroes Thursdays. So on any day of the week at least three of the four are open. Check the city dog parks page before you go.

Foothills Park Dog Park Agility + two rotating sides

Foothills Park, 57th Ave. & Union Hills Dr., 85308, Glendale, AZ · North Glendale · northeast of the softball complex, access from the Foothills Library parking lot · City of Glendale Parks & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — two separate enclosed sections that rotate monthly (signs show which side is open)
  • Water Yes — water listed for each side
  • Shade Yes — shade listed for each side
  • Small-dog area Yes — each side has dedicated large- and small-dog spaces
  • Restrooms Not stated
  • Hours Park hours 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; dog park closed Wednesdays for maintenance

Grass, shade, water, and benches on both sides, and the east side adds an agility course. The two sides rotate monthly, so check the signs when you arrive. Foothills Park also has an aquatics center, playgrounds, ramadas, picnic areas with grills, a skate court, and a sports complex. City dog parks page · Foothills Park page.

Glendale Heroes Park Dog Park Lake + splash pad park

Glendale Heroes Regional Park, 83rd Ave. & Bethany Home Rd., 85303, Glendale, AZ · West Glendale · east of the picnic ramadas, south of the archery range · City of Glendale Parks & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — two separate sections for large and small dogs, rotated monthly (signs show which side is open)
  • Water Yes — water listed for each section
  • Shade Yes — shade listed for each section
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate large- and small-dog sections
  • Restrooms Yes — restrooms listed for the park (location relative to the dog park not stated)
  • Hours Park hours daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; dog park closed Thursdays for maintenance

The dog park came with Heroes Regional Park’s newest expansion, which broke ground in May 2024 alongside eight pickleball courts, lighted multi-use fields, and a mini-pitch. Each section has grass, shade, water, and benches. The park also has a 4-acre fishing lake, a splash pad, the X-Court for skaters and bikers, an archery range, and restrooms. City dog parks page · Heroes Regional Park page.

Northern Horizon Park Dog Park Fenced, large + small

Northern Horizon Park, 63rd Ave. & Northern Ave., Glendale, AZ · Central Glendale · City of Glendale Parks & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — fenced-in facility
  • Water Yes — water fountains
  • Shade Yes — shade trees
  • Small-dog area Yes — separate areas for large and small dogs
  • Restrooms Not stated
  • Hours Closed Mondays for maintenance; the city’s dog park page doesn’t list daily hours for this park

A fenced neighborhood dog park with separate large- and small-dog areas, water fountains, shade trees, benches, and waste stations. City dog parks page.

Sahuaro Ranch Park Dog Park Agility + historic ranch

Sahuaro Ranch Park, 9802 N 59th Ave., 85302, Glendale, AZ (dog park off 63rd Ave. & Mountain View Rd.) · Central Glendale · north of the west parking lot, south of the softball complex · City of Glendale Parks & Recreation

  • Fenced Yes — gated off-leash area (the city asks visitors to close the gate behind them)
  • Water Yes — water fountains
  • Shade Not stated for the dog park
  • Small-dog area Yes — small-dog area
  • Restrooms Not stated
  • Hours Park hours daily 6 a.m.–10 p.m.; dog park closed Tuesdays for maintenance

Has a small-dog area, water fountains, waste stations, and agility equipment. Sahuaro Ranch Park is the city’s historic ranch park, with old ranch buildings, a rose garden, peacocks, playgrounds, ramadas, and a softball complex. City dog parks page · Sahuaro Ranch Park page.

FAQ — Glendale dog parks

What is the best dog park in Glendale, AZ?

Foothills Park Dog Park, at 57th Avenue and Union Hills Drive. The city lists grass, shade, water, benches, and separate large- and small-dog spaces on both of its rotating sides, plus an agility course on the east side.

How many dog parks does Glendale have?

Four city off-leash dog parks: Foothills Park, Glendale Heroes Park, Northern Horizon Park, and Sahuaro Ranch Park.

Do Glendale dog parks have small-dog areas?

Yes. The city lists a small-dog area or separate large- and small-dog sections at all four dog parks.

Which Glendale dog parks have agility equipment?

Foothills Park (an agility course on the east side) and Sahuaro Ranch Park (agility equipment).

When are Glendale dog parks closed for maintenance?

Northern Horizon: Mondays. Sahuaro Ranch: Tuesdays. Foothills: Wednesdays. Glendale Heroes: Thursdays.

What are Glendale dog park hours?

Foothills, Sahuaro Ranch, and Glendale Heroes Regional Park list park hours of 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. The city’s dog park page doesn’t give daily hours for Northern Horizon, only its Monday maintenance closure.

How many dogs can I bring to a Glendale dog park?

Up to three dogs per visit, and you must be able to control them. Dogs must be licensed, vaccinated, and wearing ID and permit tags.
