# Sources — Peoria, AZ dog parks (ParkPaws)

Researched for the Peoria city hub on 2026-11-15 (America/Los_Angeles). Only parks and amenity claims backed by the links below are published; gaps are marked "Not stated" on the page.

## Primary — City of Peoria

- Dog Parks in Peoria AZ (six locations with addresses; pet drinking fountains, Mutt Mitts, waste containers; 16 rules, PCC 7-66; Community Services (623) 773-7137): https://www.peoriaaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation-and-community-facilities/parks-and-trails/dog-parks
  (intro text says "five dog-friendly parks" but six are listed; page fetched 2026-11-15)
- Parks and Trails (park hours daily 6:00 a.m.–10:30 p.m., some holiday exceptions; list of six parks with designated dog areas): https://www.peoriaaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation-and-community-facilities/parks-and-trails
- Paloma Community Park (29799 N. Lake Pleasant Pkwy., 85383; Lake Pleasant Pkwy. & Dixileta Dr.; daily 6 a.m.–10:30 p.m.; amenities incl. "Lighted Dog Park", multiple restroom buildings): https://www.peoriaaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation-and-community-facilities/parks-and-trails/paloma-community-park
- Pioneer Community Park (8755 N. 83rd Ave., 85345; 83 acres near SE corner of 83rd Ave. & Olive Ave.; daily 6 a.m.–10:30 p.m.; amenities incl. dog park): https://www.peoriaaz.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation-and-community-facilities/parks-and-trails/pioneer-community-park
