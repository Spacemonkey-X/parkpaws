Best dog parks in Austin, TX

Official off-leash areas with fencing, water notes, hours, and algae advisories — researched from City of Austin sources.

Best dog parks in San Antonio, TX

Enclosed city dog parks with small-dog sections, water, hours, and Parks & Rec rules — researched from sa.gov.

Best dog parks in Houston, TX

HPARD and metro dog parks with fencing, ponds, small-dog runs, and dawn-to-dusk rules — researched from houstontx.gov and partners.

Best dog parks in Dallas, TX

Dallas Parks off-leash sites with fencing, water, small-dog paddocks, rain-out hotline, and city rules — researched from dallasparks.org.

Best dog parks in Fort Worth, TX

Fort Woof, ZBonz, and four neighborhood off-leash parks — fencing, ponds, small-dog areas, hours, and maintenance days from fortworthtexas.gov.

Best dog parks in Los Angeles, CA

All 13 City of LA Recreation & Parks off-leash sites with fencing, small-dog notes, sunrise–sunset hours, and LAMC rules.

Best dog parks in San Diego, CA

City leash-free pens, Dog Beach, and Fiesta Island — fencing, water stations, and hours from sandiego.gov.

Best dog parks in Long Beach, CA

Enclosed Parks Recreation & Marine dog parks plus Rosie’s Dog Beach — researched from longbeach.gov.

Best dog parks in Irvine, CA

Central Bark — Irvine’s only City off-leash park, with a fenced small-dog yard, dog watering, lighting, and Wednesday maintenance notes from cityofirvine.gov.

Best dog parks in Huntington Beach, CA

Fenced Central Park dog park with small/large sides plus off-leash Huntington Dog Beach — City parks and beach rules.

Best dog parks in Anaheim, CA

Four City dog parks — Olive Hills, La Palma, Maxwell, and Jacaranda — with small-dog areas and hours from anaheim.net.

Best dog parks in Santa Ana, CA

Santa Ana’s two new 2026 dog parks at Centennial and Mabury — small/medium/large yards from santa-ana.org.

Best dog parks in Riverside, CA

Carlson Bark Park and Riverwalk Dog Park with water and City rules, plus nearby War Dog Memorial Park (March JPA).

Best dog parks in Rancho Cucamonga, CA

The Bark at Central Park — three one-acre yards, fountains, shade, dawn–dusk hours. Etiwanda Creek is closed.

Best dog parks in Corona, CA

Butterfield and Griffin dog parks with Corona’s two-dog limit and rabies/parvo rules from coronaca.gov.

Best dog parks in San Francisco, CA

SF Rec & Park Dog Play Areas with fencing, Mission Bay water notes, Health Code leash rules, and 12 documented hubs.

Best dog parks in Oakland, CA

All 5 fenced dog play areas plus 4 official off-leash parks — researched from oaklandca.gov.

Best dog parks in San Jose, CA

12 City of San José dog parks with dual pens, water notes, and two-dog rules from sanjoseca.gov.

Best dog parks in Sacramento, CA

15 official City of Sacramento off-leash dog parks — fencing, water, small-dog yards, and YPCE rules from cityofsacramento.gov.

Colorado

Best dog parks in Denver, CO

All 17 Denver Parks & Recreation dog parks — fence type, acreage, fountains, high/low-energy areas, Railyard’s lighted hours, and the two-dog rule.

Best dog parks in Colorado Springs, CO

Fenced Antlers, Rampart, Palmer and Cheyenne Meadows dog parks, unfenced Garden of the Gods and Red Rock Canyon runs, plus county-run Bear Creek.

Best dog parks in Aurora, CO

Grandview, Bicentennial and Singletree City dog parks plus the 100-acre Cherry Creek State Park off-leash area and its pass fees.

Best dog parks in Fort Collins, CO

Fenced City dog parks at Fossil Creek, Soft Gold, Spring Canyon and Twin Silo with 5 a.m.–11 p.m. hours from fortcollins.gov.

Best dog parks in Boulder, CO

East Boulder, Foothills and Valmont fenced dog parks with small-dog yards, plus the Howard Heuston Voice & Sight area.

Best dog parks in Lakewood, CO

Forsberg-Iron Spring plus new Mountair (2024) and Wright Park (2026) off-leash dog parks from lakewoodco.gov.

Arizona

Best dog parks in Phoenix, AZ

All 13 City of Phoenix dog parks — shade, water, restrooms, Steele Indian School’s small-dog yard, and 5:30 a.m.–11 p.m. hours from phoenix.gov.

Best dog parks in Mesa, AZ

Mesa’s three city off-leash parks — Eastmark’s active and timid-dog yards, Countryside, and Quail Run (temporarily closed) — with weekly maintenance days from mesaaz.gov.

Best dog parks in Chandler, AZ

Chandler’s four fenced city dog parks — Snedigar, Paseo Vista, Shawnee, and Nozomi — with dog drinking fountains, agility obstacles, and weekly maintenance closures from chandleraz.gov.

Best dog parks in Scottsdale, AZ

Scottsdale’s four city dog parks — Chaparral, the new Thompson Peak, Horizon, and Vista del Camino — with shade, dog fountains, active/passive areas, and maintenance mornings from scottsdaleaz.gov.

Best dog parks in Gilbert, AZ

Gilbert’s two off-leash parks — Cosmo Dog Park with its dog beach and dog wash, and the quieter Crossroads Park — with fencing, fountains, active/timid areas, and closures from gilbertaz.gov.

Best dog parks in Tempe, AZ

All seven free Tempe dog parks — led by the new Kiwanis Dog Park with its small-dog pod, agility pod, water spray, and shade — with hours and weekly maintenance closures from tempe.gov.
